# Scenario Comparisons

### Overview

This guide explains how to **compare multiple scenarios side-by-side** in Agentnoon, enabling you to analyze differences in workforce structure, costs, and headcount.

#### 1. Accessing Scenario Comparison

* On the home page, you will see the scenarios you have created.
* Choose **two scenarios** to compare.&#x20;
* Click **Compare** (bottom-right corner).
* Example: Compare **Scenario A** with **Scenario B**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-08 at 2.28.16 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 2. Selecting Scenarios for Comparison

* A **side-by-side view** will display scenario differences.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-08 at 2.21.19 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 3. Customizing the Comparison View

* Use the **Select Dropdown** to choose comparison metrics:
  * **Cost** comparison.
  * **Headcount** differences.
  * **Pyramid Chart** (organizational structure visualization).

#### 4. Comparing More Than Two Scenarios

* Use the **arrow keys** at the bottom to add additional scenarios.
* Compare **up to four scenarios** side-by-side for deeper insights.

By using **scenario comparison**, you can **evaluate workforce planning decisions, assess financial impacts, and optimize organizational changes** in Agentnoon.
