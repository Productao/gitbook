# Scenario Management&#x20;

### Overview

This guide covers how you can rename, duplicate, and delete, scenarios in Agentnoon.

### Duplicate, Rename, or Share Scenarios

You can rename, duplicate, or share a scenario from the **Actions** menu.

1. Navigate to **All Scenarios** by clicking on the dropdown next to the project name.
2. Locate the scenario you want to manage and select  the following options under **Actions.**\
   ![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png>)**Rename** – Update the scenario title. \
   ![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (13).png>)**Duplicate** – Create a copy to explore alternate plans.\
   ![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png>)**Share** - Invite other team members to this scenario.

### Deleting a Scenario

1. Locate the scenario you want to manage and click the **three-dot menu (⋮)**.
   * By clicking **Delete**, you will permanently remove the scenario from your list.\
