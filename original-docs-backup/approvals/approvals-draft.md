# Approvals draft

### Overview

Approval flows ensure that changes made in a scenario go through the necessary review process before implementation. Scenario creators can define a structured approval flow with two levels of approvers, keeping decision-making streamlined and transparent.

### 1. Setting Up Approvals

* After making changes in a scenario, click the Configure Approvals button to assign approvers before submission.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 2.38.16 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* There are **two levels of approvals**, which the scenario creator defines.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 2.36.22 AM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* Approvers receive notifications to review the scenario.

### 2. Locking a Scenario

* Optionally, check the box to lock the scenario after submission to prevent further edits.
* Locked scenarios can only be viewed and approved—reviewers cannot make changes.

### 3. Approving a Scenario

* Approvers can review the scenario details but **cannot edit** them.
* Approval must be granted at both levels for the scenario to proceed.
* Once fully approved, the scenario is finalized and ready for implementation.
