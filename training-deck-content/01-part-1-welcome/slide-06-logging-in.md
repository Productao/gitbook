# Slide 6 — Logging In & the Homepage

## Title
Logging In & the Homepage

## Lead-line
Your home base for accessing your organization and managing scenarios.

## Key Details
- **Log in** at app.agentnoon.com using your credentials (email/password, Google SSO, Microsoft SSO, or Okta — depending on your organization's setup)
- **Homepage shows:** Your organization instance name at the top, a list of your scenarios in the main panel, and quick links to documentation
- **Open Org Chart** button takes you into Main Org to explore your current organization
- **Scenario list** is where you'll create, open, and manage all your planning work — think of it as your project dashboard

## Speaker Notes
The homepage is intentionally simple. Your scenarios live here — you'll come back to this screen every time you want to start new planning work or pick up where you left off. If this is your first time logging in, the scenario list may be empty. That's normal — we'll create your first scenario shortly. For now, let's click "Open Org Chart" to explore your organization.
