# Slide 3 — What is Agentnoon?

## Title
What is Agentnoon?

## Lead-line
A workforce planning platform that turns your org data into clear insights and confident decisions.

## Key Details
- **Visualize your organization** — See your entire org structure, reporting lines, and workforce data in one place
- **Model changes safely** — Run "what-if" scenarios without affecting your live data
- **Forecast impact** — Project headcount and cost over time to see how plans play out before you commit
- **Share insights** — Export charts, reports, and comparisons to align stakeholders and drive approvals

## Speaker Notes
Agentnoon is built for People teams, Finance teams, and organizational leaders who need to plan workforce changes with confidence. Instead of modeling reorgs in spreadsheets or slide decks, you work directly with your real org data. Every change you model is tracked, costed, and time-phased — so you always know the impact before you commit. The platform syncs with your HRIS so you're always working from current data.
