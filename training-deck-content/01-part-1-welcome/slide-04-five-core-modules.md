# Slide 4 — The Five Core Modules

## Title
The Five Core Modules

## Lead-line
Agentnoon is organized into five modules — each serves a different purpose, but they all work from the same data.

## Key Details
- **Main Org** — Your current, live organizational structure. View-only. Reflects data from your HRIS or import. This is your baseline.
- **Scenarios** — Editable copies of your org where you model future changes. Add, move, edit, or close positions without affecting live data.
- **Forecast** — Headcount and cost projections over time. Shows how scenario changes phase in by month, quarter, or year.
- **Workforce Hub** — Pre-built analytics charts for structural analysis. Span of control, headcount distribution, heatmaps, and more.
- **Directory** — Table/spreadsheet view of all positions and employees. Sort, filter, bulk edit (in scenarios), and export.

## Speaker Notes
Think of these five modules as different lenses on the same data. Main Org shows you where you are today. Scenarios let you plan where you want to go. Forecast tells you what that costs over time. Workforce Hub helps you analyze structural health. And Directory gives you the spreadsheet view for detailed analysis and exports. You'll use all five — and they all stay in sync because they share the same underlying data.
