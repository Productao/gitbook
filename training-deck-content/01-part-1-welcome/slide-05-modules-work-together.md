# Slide 5 — How the Modules Work Together

## Title
How the Modules Work Together

## Lead-line
A typical workflow moves through the modules in a natural progression — from understanding, to planning, to communicating.

## Key Details
- **Step 1: Main Org** — Understand your current state. Explore reporting lines, search for positions, review your baseline.
- **Step 2: Workforce Hub** — Analyze structure and identify opportunities. Check span of control, headcount distribution, and org depth.
- **Step 3: Scenarios** — Model proposed changes. Add, move, edit, or close positions to design your future org.
- **Step 4: Forecast** — Project headcount and cost over time. See how changes phase in and validate against budget targets.
- **Step 5: Directory / Export** — Export detailed data, charts, and comparisons for stakeholder reporting and approvals.

## Speaker Notes
This is the most common workflow, but it's not rigid. You might jump between modules depending on your task. For example, while building a scenario you might switch to Workforce Hub to check if your reorg is creating span-of-control issues, then switch to Forecast to see the cost impact, then go back to the scenario to make adjustments. The key point is that all modules work together — data flows seamlessly between them.
