# Slide 11 — Spans & Layers

## Title
Spans & Layers

## Lead-line
Two auto-calculated metrics that tell you how your organization is structured — breadth and depth at a glance.

## Key Details
- **Span of Control (SOC)** = The number of direct reports for a given manager. A healthy range is 5–10 for most roles. SOC below 3 suggests management compression (too many managers for too few people). SOC above 15 suggests a manager may be overburdened.
- **Layers** = The number of management levels between a position and the CEO. Industry best practice is 4–7 layers depending on org size. Too many layers can slow decision-making; too few can overload managers.
- **Both are automatic:** Agentnoon calculates SOC and Layers for every position and updates them in real time as you make changes in scenarios.
- **Why they matter:** These are the two metrics you'll check most often when validating a reorg — they tell you whether your structure is balanced and efficient.

## Speaker Notes
Span of Control and Layers are the bread and butter of org design. When you're doing a reorg, these two numbers tell you whether you're creating a healthy structure. If you see a bunch of managers with only 1–2 direct reports, that's compression — you're probably paying for management overhead without getting the value. If you see someone with 20+ reports, they're likely a bottleneck. We'll use these metrics throughout this training, especially when we get to Workforce Hub analytics.
