# Slide 9 — Cards & the Org Chart

## Title
Cards & the Org Chart

## Lead-line
In the org chart, each position appears as a card — the visual building block of your organization.

## Key Details
- **Default display:** Each card shows the employee name (if filled) and job title. Vacant positions show the title only with a blank name area.
- **Customizable:** Use the Card Content tool to add more fields to every card — department, salary, location, span of control, or any other attribute. Changes apply instantly to all cards.
- **Interacting with cards:** Hover to see quick details. Click to open the full detail panel. In scenarios, hover to see action buttons (add, edit, close, duplicate).
- **FX fields:** Cards can display auto-calculated metrics marked with "FX" — like Span of Control, Total Org Size, and Layer — giving you structural insight directly on the org chart.

## Speaker Notes
Cards are how you interact with your org chart. By default they're minimal — just name and title — but you can load them up with as much data as you need. One of the most useful things to add is Span of Control, which tells you how many direct reports each manager has. We'll cover how to customize card content in the Main Org section. For now, just remember: one card = one position.
