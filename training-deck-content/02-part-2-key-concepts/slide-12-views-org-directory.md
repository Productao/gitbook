# Slide 12 — Views: Org Chart vs Directory

## Title
Two Views, Same Data: Org Chart & Directory

## Lead-line
Switch between a visual hierarchy and a sortable spreadsheet anytime — your filters travel with you.

## Key Details
- **Org Chart view** = Visual, hierarchical display of reporting relationships. Best for understanding structure, visualizing teams, and drag-and-drop changes in scenarios. Think of it as the "map" of your organization.
- **Directory view** = Table/spreadsheet format with sortable columns. Best for bulk analysis, sorting and filtering data, finding specific records, and exporting structured reports. Think of it as the "database" of your organization.
- **Switch anytime:** Use the view switcher or keyboard shortcuts — press **1** for Org Chart, **2** for Directory. Your active filters persist when you switch.
- **Use both together:** Start in the org chart to get the visual picture, then switch to Directory when you need to analyze, sort, or export data in bulk.

## Speaker Notes
You'll develop a preference over time, but most users work primarily in the org chart for visual exploration and restructuring, then switch to Directory for data analysis and exports. The important thing to know is that these aren't separate data sets — they're two views of the exact same information. If you filter to "Engineering Department" in the org chart and then switch to Directory, you'll see only Engineering positions in the table.
