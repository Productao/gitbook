# Slide 8 — Main Org vs Scenarios

## Title
Main Org vs Scenarios

## Lead-line
Main Org is your current reality. Scenarios are your sandbox for planning the future.

## Key Details
- **Main Org** = Your current-state organization as it exists today. It's view-only — you cannot edit it directly. It reflects real-time or imported data from your HRIS and serves as the baseline for all scenario planning.
- **Scenarios** = Editable copies of your org used for modeling future changes. You can add, move, edit, and close positions freely. Nothing you do in a scenario touches Main Org.
- **Isolation:** Scenarios are completely isolated from Main Org. Changes only become real when they're implemented in your HRIS externally and the data is refreshed.
- **Multiple scenarios:** You can create unlimited scenarios to explore different approaches — then compare them side-by-side to find the best path forward.

## Speaker Notes
Think of Main Org as the "source of truth" — it's what your organization actually looks like today. Scenarios are where all the planning happens. You can create as many as you want: "Q2 Hiring Plan," "Engineering Reorg Option A," "Budget Cut Scenario." None of these affect your real data until you deliberately implement the changes outside of Agentnoon and refresh. This means you can experiment fearlessly.
