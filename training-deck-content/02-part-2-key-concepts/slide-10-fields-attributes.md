# Slide 10 — Fields & Attributes

## Title
Fields & Attributes

## Lead-line
Fields are the data points that power everything you see in Agentnoon — from cards to charts to exports.

## Key Details
- **Position fields** are tied to the role and persist even when vacant. Examples: Job Title, Department, Pay Grade, Location, Reports To. These define the org structure.
- **People fields** are tied to the individual and are removed when someone leaves. Examples: Employee Name, Employee ID, Start Date, Email, Performance Rating.
- **FX (calculated) fields** are computed automatically by Agentnoon and update in real time. Examples: Span of Control, Layer, Total Org Size, Average SOC.
- **Where fields appear:** On cards, in the Directory, in Workforce Hub charts, in filters and highlights, and in all exports. The same data powers every view.

## Speaker Notes
The distinction between position fields and people fields matters when you're planning. Position fields like "Department" and "Pay Grade" stick with the role no matter who's in it. People fields like "Name" and "Start Date" travel with the person. So if you detach an employee from a position, the position keeps its title and department, but the employee's name and salary go to the bench. This separation is what makes position-based planning so powerful — you can design roles independently of the people who fill them.
