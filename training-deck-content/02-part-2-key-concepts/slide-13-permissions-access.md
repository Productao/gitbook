# Slide 13 — Permissions & Access

## Title
Permissions & Access

## Lead-line
Your admin controls what you can see and do — here's a quick overview of how access works.

## Key Details
- **Viewer** — Can see org data and scenarios shared with them. Cannot edit or create scenarios. Ideal for leadership and stakeholders who need visibility.
- **Planner** — Can create and edit scenarios, model changes, and export data. The standard role for HR Business Partners, workforce planners, and department leads.
- **Approver** — Can approve or reject submitted scenarios. Typically assigned to senior leaders, Finance, or HR leadership.
- **Admin** — Full system access: user management, data uploads, field configuration, access groups, approval workflows, and all platform settings.
- **Access Groups** control which data you can see, scoped by department, location, or other attributes. You only see positions within your assigned scope.

## Speaker Notes
Most users in Agentnoon are Planners — they can create scenarios and model changes. Your admin has set up your access to show the data relevant to your role. If you can't see certain departments or positions, that's by design — your access group is scoped to the data you need. If you think you're missing access to something, reach out to your admin.
