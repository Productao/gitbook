# Slide U4 — Use Case: M&A Integration

## Title
Use Case: M&A Integration

## Lead-line
Plan post-acquisition organizational integration — from data import to leadership consolidation to cost synergy analysis.

## Goal
Model the combined organization after an acquisition, identify redundancies and synergies, plan the integration timeline, and present multiple approaches to executive leadership.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### 1. Set Up the Combined Org
- Create a **Full Org** scenario from your organization
- Upload the acquired company's data via **Partial Data Upload** (Appendix A8) — include their positions, reporting structure, departments, and compensation data
- Verify the combined org appears correctly in the scenario

### 2. Plan Leadership Consolidation
- Identify overlapping executive and management roles
- Use drag-and-drop or Change Manager to restructure reporting lines
- Close redundant leadership positions (RIF) — track cost savings in the OpEx Panel
- Consider using the **Bench** for individuals who need role reassignment

### 3. Integrate Departments
- Identify redundant functions across both organizations
- Consolidate teams: merge overlapping departments, align naming conventions using bulk edit
- Use **Highlight** by the source company (create a custom field) to visualize which positions come from which organization

### 4. Analyze Impact
- **OpEx Panel:** Track total cost synergies (savings from eliminated redundancies)
- **Workforce Hub:** Check structural health — SOC, layers, headcount distribution post-integration
- **Forecast:** Set effective dates to phase the integration over time

### 5. Compare Approaches
- Create multiple scenarios representing different integration speeds:
  - **Fast (3 months)** — Aggressive consolidation, maximum cost synergies, higher disruption risk
  - **Moderate (6 months)** — Balanced approach, phased consolidation
  - **Slow (12 months)** — Gradual integration, minimal disruption, delayed synergies
- Run **Comparisons** to present cost savings vs. timeline trade-offs

### 6. Present to Leadership
- Export org chart comparisons (before/after) as PowerPoint
- Export cost synergy analysis from OpEx Panel
- Export phased Forecast showing when savings materialize

## Key Metrics to Check
- Total combined headcount (before and after integration)
- Cost synergies (savings from redundancy elimination)
- Number of redundant positions identified
- SOC and layer distribution post-integration
- Integration timeline and phased cost impact

## Speaker Notes
M&A integration is one of the most complex use cases in Agentnoon but also one of the most valuable. The Partial Data Upload feature is key — it lets you bring the acquired company's org data into a scenario without disrupting your Main Org. The multi-scenario approach (Fast/Moderate/Slow) is essential for executive presentations because every acquisition involves a trade-off between speed-of-savings and disruption-risk. Present the options with data, and let leadership choose the approach that fits their risk tolerance and business context.
