# Slide U1 — Use Case: Planning a Reorganization

## Title
Use Case: Planning a Reorganization

## Lead-line
Model structural changes before implementing them — from analysis to approval.

## Goal
Redesign part or all of your organizational structure to improve efficiency, reduce management layers, or align teams to new strategic priorities.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### 1. Analyze Current State
- Open **Main Org** → add **Span of Control** and **Layer** to Card Content
- Use **Workforce Hub** → Layers & Spans chart to identify compression (SOC 1–2) and overload (SOC 15+)
- Use **Spotlight** to highlight positions outside healthy SOC ranges (5–10)

### 2. Define Goals
- Set measurable targets: "Reduce layers from 8 to 6," "Eliminate managers with fewer than 3 reports," "Move QA under Product"
- Document goals in the scenario description for reviewer context

### 3. Create a Scenario
- **Partial Org** for a specific department, or **Full Org** for company-wide restructuring
- Name clearly: "Engineering Q2 2026 Reorg — Option A"

### 4. Model Changes
- **Move teams** to new managers using drag-and-drop or Change Manager
- **Close redundant positions** (RIF or Exit)
- **Add new positions** where needed to fill structural gaps
- **Bulk edit** department fields for moved teams

### 5. Review Impact
- **OpEx Panel:** Check net headcount and cost impact
- **Workforce Hub:** Compare Before/After on Layers & Spans chart — did you actually reduce compression?
- **Forecast:** Verify phasing if you used effective dates

### 6. Compare Alternatives
- Duplicate the scenario to create Option B with a different approach
- Run a **Comparison** from the homepage to present both options

### 7. Submit for Approval
- Write a clear justification referencing your goals and the structural improvements
- Export charts and comparisons for pre-socialization with key stakeholders

## Key Metrics to Check
- Span of Control distribution (before vs. after)
- Number of organizational layers
- Manager-to-IC ratio
- Headcount by department

## Speaker Notes
Reorgs are the most common use case in Agentnoon. The key to a successful reorg is starting with data — use Workforce Hub to identify the structural problems, set clear targets, and then measure your scenario against those targets before submitting. Creating 2–3 alternative scenarios and comparing them gives leadership options rather than a single take-it-or-leave-it proposal.
