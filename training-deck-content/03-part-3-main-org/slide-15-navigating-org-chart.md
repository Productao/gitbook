# Slide 15 — Navigating the Org Chart

## Title
Navigating the Org Chart

## Lead-line
Scroll, zoom, expand, and collapse — here's how to move around your organization.

## Key Details
- **Scroll and zoom:** Use your mouse or trackpad to pan around the org chart and zoom in/out. Pinch-to-zoom works on trackpads.
- **Expand / Collapse:** Click the arrow on any card to show or hide its direct reports. This lets you drill into specific parts of the org without seeing everything at once.
- **Layout options:** Choose between Vertical (top-down), Horizontal (left-to-right), and Compact views. Each layout works better for different org shapes.
- **Expansion levels (1–3):** Control how many levels deep the org chart expands by default. Level 1 shows only the top level; Level 3 shows three levels down.
- **Fit to screen:** Use the fit-to-screen button to reset your view and see the full visible org at once.

## Speaker Notes
Navigation is intuitive once you try it. The most common action is expanding and collapsing — click the arrow on a manager's card to see their team, click again to collapse it. If you have a very large org, start with expansion level 1 and drill into specific areas. The layout options are useful for different situations: Vertical is the standard org chart view, Horizontal works well for wide teams, and Compact fits more on screen. Experiment to find what works for your org.
