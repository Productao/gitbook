# Slide 20 — Main Org: Key Takeaways

## Title
Main Org: Key Takeaways

## Lead-line
You now know how to explore your current organization — next, we'll start planning changes.

## Key Details
- **Main Org is view-only** — Explore freely, but make changes in Scenarios
- **Search** to find any person or position instantly (Cmd/Ctrl+K)
- **Filter** to narrow the org chart to specific departments, locations, or attributes
- **Highlight** to color-code cards by any field and surface patterns visually
- **Card Content** to control which data appears on every card — add SOC, salary, department, or any field
- **Spotlight** to dim non-matching positions and focus on specific criteria
- **Saved Views** to bookmark your favorite configurations for quick access
- **Next up:** Creating your first Scenario and making changes

## Speaker Notes
Before we move on, take a moment to explore Main Org on your own if you can. Try searching for yourself or a colleague, filtering to your department, and highlighting by location. Get comfortable with the navigation — everything you've learned here also works inside Scenarios. The key difference is that Scenarios are editable, which means all these tools become even more powerful when combined with the ability to make changes. Let's jump in.
