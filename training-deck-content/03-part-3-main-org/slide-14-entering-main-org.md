# Slide 14 — Entering Main Org

## Title
Entering Main Org

## Lead-line
Main Org is your starting point — a live, view-only snapshot of your current organization.

## Key Details
- **How to get there:** Click "Open Org Chart" from the homepage. You'll land in Main Org by default.
- **View-only:** You cannot add, edit, or delete positions in Main Org. It reflects your current-state data imported from your HRIS or data source.
- **Single source of truth:** Main Org is the baseline for every scenario you create. When you create a scenario, it starts as a copy of Main Org (or a filtered subset of it).
- **Start here:** Before you plan any changes, spend time exploring Main Org to understand your current structure — reporting lines, team sizes, and organizational depth.

## Speaker Notes
Every time you open Agentnoon and click "Open Org Chart," you're looking at Main Org. This is the real state of your organization based on the last data sync. You can explore freely — click around, search, filter, highlight — but you can't change anything here. That's intentional: Main Org is the foundation for all your planning. Changes happen in Scenarios, which we'll get to shortly. For now, let's learn how to navigate and find what you need.
