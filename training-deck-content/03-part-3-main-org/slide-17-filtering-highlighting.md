# Slide 17 — Filtering & Highlighting

## Title
Filtering & Highlighting

## Lead-line
Focus on specific parts of your org with filters, and use color-coding to surface patterns instantly.

## Key Details
- **Filtering:** Click **Filter** in the toolbar → select criteria (Department, Location, Pay Grade, etc.) → click **Apply**. Only positions matching your criteria remain visible.
  - Turn on **"Preserve manager relationships"** to keep the reporting chain visible even when managers don't match the filter — this gives you context.
- **Highlighting:** Click **Highlight** in the toolbar → choose an attribute (Department, Work Country, Pay Grade, etc.) → cards automatically color-code by value with a legend.
  - **Fill Color** changes the card background; **Outline Color** adds a colored border instead.
- **Combine both:** Apply a filter to narrow to a specific team, then add highlighting to color-code by location or pay grade within that team.

## Speaker Notes
Filtering and highlighting are your primary analysis tools in the org chart. A common workflow: filter to a specific department, then highlight by location to see where the team is distributed geographically. Or filter to all managers, then highlight by span of control to quickly spot who has too many or too few reports. The "Preserve manager relationships" option is especially useful — without it, you only see the matching positions. With it, you see the full chain from the CEO down, so you understand where those positions sit in the hierarchy.
