# Slide 16 — Searching for People & Positions

## Title
Searching for People & Positions

## Lead-line
Find anyone or any role in seconds — search by name, title, ID, or any attribute.

## Key Details
- **Open search:** Click the search bar in the toolbar, or use the keyboard shortcut **Cmd/Ctrl+K** (or just press **/**)
- **What you can search:** Employee name, job title, employee ID, position ID, or any other text attribute in your data
- **Live results:** Results appear as you type — no need to press Enter. The list narrows with each character.
- **Navigate directly:** Click any search result to jump straight to that card in the org chart. The view zooms in and centers on the selected position.

## Screenshot / Visual
Search bar open with a partial query typed and the results dropdown visible, showing matching employees/positions with name and title.

## Speaker Notes
Search is the fastest way to find anyone in your org. If you know the person's name, start typing it. If you're looking for a role, type the job title. If you have an employee ID or position ID from a spreadsheet, paste it in. The search covers all data within your access scope — so if you can't find someone, they may be outside your access group. One tip: the keyboard shortcut Cmd+K (or Ctrl+K on Windows) opens search from anywhere in the platform, even when you're inside a scenario.
