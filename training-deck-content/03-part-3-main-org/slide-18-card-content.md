# Slide 18 — Customizing Card Content

## Title
Customizing Card Content

## Lead-line
Control exactly what information appears on every card — add the fields that matter most to your analysis.

## Key Details
- **Open Card Content:** Click the **Card Content** button in the toolbar. A panel appears with all available fields.
- **Add or remove fields:** Check or uncheck any field to display it on cards. Common additions: Department, Salary, Location, Pay Grade, Employee Type.
- **FX fields:** Include auto-calculated fields like **Span of Control**, **Total Org Size**, or **Layer** to see structural metrics directly on every card.
- **Reorder fields:** Click the **gear button** in the top-right of the Card Content panel to drag and rearrange the display order of fields on cards.
- **Instant updates:** Changes apply immediately to all visible cards — no need to save or refresh.

## Speaker Notes
Card Content is one of the features you'll use constantly. The default view — just name and title — is clean but doesn't tell you much. Adding a few key fields transforms the org chart into an analytical tool. For example, add Salary and Department and suddenly you can visually scan compensation across teams. Add Span of Control and Layer and you can spot structural issues just by scrolling through the chart. The fields you choose will depend on your task — a budget review needs different data visible than an org design exercise.
