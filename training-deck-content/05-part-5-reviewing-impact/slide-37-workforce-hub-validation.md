# Slide 37 — Validating with Workforce Hub

## Title
Validating with Workforce Hub

## Lead-line
Use analytics charts to sanity-check your scenario — are you creating a healthy org structure or introducing new problems?

## Key Details
- **How to access:** Click the view switcher and select **Workforce Hub** (or press keyboard shortcut **4**) while inside your scenario
- **Before / After toggle:** Workforce Hub charts in scenarios support Before/After comparison — see how your changes affect structural metrics side by side
- **Key charts to check:**
  - **Layers & Spans of Control** — Are you creating management compression (SOC 1–2) or overloading managers (SOC 15+)? Is the org getting deeper or flatter?
  - **Headcount Distribution** — Is the org shape healthy? A bottom-heavy pyramid is typical and healthy. Top-heavy or diamond shapes may need attention.
- **Catch issues early:** If your reorg accidentally creates a manager with 25 direct reports or adds unnecessary layers, you'll see it here before you submit for approval
- **Export for presentations:** Charts can be exported as PNG or PowerPoint for stakeholder decks

## Speaker Notes
Workforce Hub is your quality check before you submit a scenario. The org chart shows you the individual changes, the OpEx Panel shows you the cost impact, and Forecast shows you the timing — but Workforce Hub shows you the structural health. Did your reorg actually flatten the org like you intended? Did it introduce compression in one area while fixing overload in another? These are the kinds of insights that charts reveal instantly. Always check Workforce Hub before submitting — it's the difference between "we moved some boxes around" and "we improved our organizational efficiency."
