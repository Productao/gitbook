# Slide 38 — Impact Review: Key Takeaways

## Title
Impact Review: Key Takeaways

## Lead-line
Three tools, one complete picture — use all three before submitting any scenario for approval.

## Key Details
- **OpEx Panel** — Your real-time change tracker. Shows every addition, reduction, and modification with cost and headcount impact. Check it continuously while building your scenario.
- **Forecast** — Your time-phased projection. Shows how headcount and cost evolve over months, quarters, and years. Validates that your phasing is realistic and budget-aligned.
- **Workforce Hub** — Your structural validation. Shows whether your changes create a healthy org — balanced spans of control, appropriate layers, and healthy headcount distribution.
- **Review all three before submitting:** OpEx Panel answers "what changed and how much does it cost?" Forecast answers "when does it happen?" Workforce Hub answers "is the resulting structure healthy?"
- **Next up:** Sharing your scenario with collaborators and submitting for approval.

## Speaker Notes
These three tools are the "review trinity" of Agentnoon. Think of it as a pre-submission checklist: (1) Check the OpEx Panel — does the cost and headcount impact match your goals? (2) Check Forecast — are changes phased correctly over time? (3) Check Workforce Hub — does the resulting structure make sense? If all three look good, you're ready to share with your team and submit for approval. If something's off, you know exactly where to go back and adjust.
