# Slide 35 — Viewing Impact in Forecast

## Title
Viewing Impact in Forecast

## Lead-line
Forecast shows how your scenario plays out over time — headcount and cost projections by month, quarter, or year.

## Key Details
- **How to access:** Click the view switcher and select **Forecast** (or press keyboard shortcut **3**)
- **What you see:** A timeline showing projected headcount or cost across your chosen time period. Changes appear based on their hire dates, termination dates, and effective dates.
- **Toggle metrics:** Switch between **Headcount** (position count) and **Cost** (salary-based projections). Hover over any data point for a detailed breakdown.
- **Before / After / Changes:** Use these toggles to compare your baseline (Main Org) against your planned state, or to see just the delta — what your scenario adds or subtracts over time.
- **Time-phased story:** Forecast turns your scenario into a narrative — "In Q1, we add 5 engineers. In Q2, we reduce 3 redundant roles. By Q3, we've hit our target headcount."

## Speaker Notes
Forecast is where your scenario comes to life as a time-phased plan. The org chart shows you the end state — what the org looks like after all changes. Forecast shows you the journey — how you get there over time. This is particularly important for stakeholders who care about budget pacing and timing. A CFO doesn't just want to know "we're adding 15 people" — they want to know when, and what it costs each quarter. The Before/After/Changes toggle is powerful: "Show Changes" shows just the net impact of your scenario — the positions you're adding minus the ones you're closing, over time.
