# Slide 36 — Configuring Forecast

## Title
Configuring Forecast

## Lead-line
Group by department, toggle between headcount and cost, and choose your time horizon — Forecast adapts to your question.

## Key Details
- **Row Aggregator:** Choose how to group data — by Department, Location, Pay Grade, People (individual), Employee Type, or any custom field. This controls the rows in your forecast table.
- **Time Period:** Select **Monthly** (precise timing, good for near-term), **Quarterly** (standard for budget reviews and board reporting), or **Yearly** (strategic, up to 5 years out).
- **Metric toggle:** Switch between **Headcount** (count of positions) and **Cost** (annual salary divided across the time period). You can also view individual compensation components like Bonus, Stock, and Allowances if configured.
- **Before / After / Changes:**
  - **Show Before** = Main Org baseline (what exists today)
  - **Show After** = Your scenario's final planned state
  - **Show Changes** = The delta only (net additions minus reductions)
- **Export:** Click Export to download the forecast table as CSV for offline analysis in Excel

## Screenshot / Visual
Forecast configuration panel showing the aggregator selector, time period options (Monthly/Quarterly/Yearly), Headcount/Cost toggle, and Before/After/Changes buttons.

## Speaker Notes
Forecast is a pivot table for your org. The Row Aggregator is the key setting — it determines what you're comparing. "By Department" shows each department's headcount over time. "By Location" shows geographic distribution. "By People" shows individual positions. The time period depends on your audience: Monthly for recruiting and operational teams, Quarterly for Finance and budget reviews, Yearly for strategic presentations to the board. One tip: always try the "Show Changes" view when reviewing a scenario — it strips away the baseline and shows only your net impact, which is usually what decision-makers want to see.
