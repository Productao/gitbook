# Slide 33 — The OpEx Panel

## Title
The OpEx Panel

## Lead-line
Your real-time dashboard for every change in a scenario — see the cost and headcount impact as you work.

## Key Details
- **What it shows:** A running summary of all additions, reductions, and modifications in your scenario — with net headcount change and net cost impact
- **Organized by change type:** Additions (new positions and their total cost), Reductions (closed positions and their savings), Data Changes (modifications like salary adjustments and their cost delta)
- **Click to navigate:** Click any change in the OpEx Panel to jump directly to that position in the org chart — useful for reviewing specific changes in context
- **Always visible:** The OpEx Panel sits on the side of your scenario workspace. Check it frequently to track progress against your goals (e.g., "I need to cut $500K — am I there yet?")

## Screenshot / Visual
The OpEx Panel open and visible, showing: total additions count and cost, total reductions count and savings, net headcount change, and net cost impact. Ideally with a few line items visible under each category.

## Speaker Notes
The OpEx Panel is the single most important tool for understanding the impact of your scenario. Every time you add, edit, move, or close a position, the numbers update in real time. If you're working toward a specific target — say, "reduce headcount by 10 and save $800K" — the OpEx Panel tells you exactly where you stand at any moment. Get in the habit of glancing at it after every few changes. It's also what your approvers will review when you submit the scenario, so make sure the numbers tell a clear story.
