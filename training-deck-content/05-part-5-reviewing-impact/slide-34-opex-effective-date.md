# Slide 34 — OpEx Panel: Effective Date View

## Title
OpEx Panel: Effective Date View

## Lead-line
See your changes grouped by when they take effect — essential for phased rollouts and budget pacing.

## Key Details
- **Toggle to Effective Date view:** Within the OpEx Panel, switch from the default category view (Additions / Reductions / Changes) to the Effective Date view. Changes are now grouped by the month or quarter they take effect.
- **Phased visibility:** See exactly what happens each period — e.g., "3 hires in March, 2 hires in April, 1 RIF in June." This helps you validate that your phasing is realistic and aligns with budget cycles.
- **Budget pacing:** If you have a quarterly budget target, this view lets you verify you're not front-loading all your costs into Q1 or back-loading them into Q4.
- **Download change data:** Use the Activity Log to export a detailed CSV of all changes — useful for offline review, stakeholder presentations, or audit documentation.

## Screenshot / Visual
The OpEx Panel in Effective Date view, showing changes grouped by month or quarter with costs for each period.

## Speaker Notes
The Effective Date view is a game-changer for phased planning. Most organizations don't make all their changes on a single day — hires are staggered, reductions are phased, moves happen at the start of a quarter. This view shows you the timeline of your plan right in the OpEx Panel. If something looks wrong — like all 20 hires showing up in January when you meant to spread them across Q1 and Q2 — you can spot it here and fix the dates before submitting. The Activity Log export gives you a complete audit trail of every change, which is useful for Finance reviews and compliance.
