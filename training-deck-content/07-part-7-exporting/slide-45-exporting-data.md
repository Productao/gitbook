# Slide 45 — Exporting Data from Directory & Forecast

## Title
Exporting Data from Directory & Forecast

## Lead-line
Get structured data out of Agentnoon for spreadsheet analysis, Finance reviews, and detailed reporting.

## Key Details
- **Directory export:** Switch to Directory view → apply filters and customize columns to show exactly the data you need → click **Export** → choose **CSV or Excel**. The export includes only visible columns and rows matching your active filters.
- **Forecast export:** In Forecast view, configure your aggregator, time period, and metric → click **Export** → download as **CSV**. The export matches your current view — same grouping, same time periods, same metric.
- **Change Summary export:** From the OpEx Panel → download the **Activity Log** as a CSV. Lists every change with details: position, change type, old value, new value, cost impact. Perfect for audit trails and Finance reviews.
- **Pro tip:** Always apply filters before exporting. Exporting everything when you only need Engineering data creates unnecessary cleanup work.

## Speaker Notes
Data exports are how you get Agentnoon data into the tools your stakeholders already use — Excel, Google Sheets, or whatever your Finance team works with. The Directory export is great for position-level data: rosters, headcount lists, compensation data. The Forecast export gives you the time-phased projections that Finance needs for budgeting. The Change Summary from the OpEx Panel is essential for approvals — it documents every change you made in the scenario. A best practice: include the export date and scenario name in your filename so you can track which version of the plan the data came from.
