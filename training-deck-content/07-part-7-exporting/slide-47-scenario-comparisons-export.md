# Slide 47 — Scenario Comparisons Export

## Title
Scenario Comparisons Export

## Lead-line
Present Option A vs Option B to decision-makers with a side-by-side comparison they can review in Excel or PowerPoint.

## Key Details
- **How to compare:** From the homepage → select exactly two scenarios → click **Compare**. You can also compare a scenario against Main Org (proposed vs. current state).
- **What you can compare:** Cost impact, Headcount impact, Org Chart structure (side-by-side), Pyramid Chart, and Key Metrics (SOC, layers, manager ratios).
- **Export formats:**
  - **Excel** — Multi-sheet workbook with detailed comparisons across metrics (ideal for Finance review)
  - **CSV** — Flat data export for custom analysis
  - **PowerPoint** — Side-by-side org chart comparison (ideal for leadership presentations)
- **When to use:** Before making a final decision on a plan. Create two versions (e.g., "Conservative Hiring" vs. "Aggressive Hiring"), compare them, and present the options to leadership with data.

## Screenshot / Visual
The scenario comparison view showing two scenarios side-by-side with key metrics compared (headcount, cost, SOC). Export button visible.

## Speaker Notes
Scenario comparisons are one of Agentnoon's most powerful features for decision-making. Instead of presenting one plan and asking "is this okay?", you present two or three options with clear trade-offs. "Option A adds 15 people and costs $2.1M. Option B adds 8 people and costs $1.3M. Here's what each looks like structurally." The Excel export is particularly useful because it gives Finance everything they need — multiple sheets with cost breakdowns, headcount comparisons, and metric summaries. The PowerPoint export puts the org charts side by side, which is great for visual learners in leadership.
