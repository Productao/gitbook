# Slide 44 — Exporting from the Org Chart

## Title
Exporting from the Org Chart

## Lead-line
Get your org chart into a presentation in seconds — export as JPEG for quick sharing or PowerPoint for full customization.

## Key Details
- **How:** Click **Export** in the toolbar → choose your format
- **JPEG** — A quick screenshot of the current org chart view, exactly as it appears on screen. Best for: Slack messages, quick emails, embedding in documents.
- **PowerPoint** — A structured, multi-slide export with configurable options:
  - **Filter scope:** Export only the filtered subset or the full visible org
  - **Hierarchy depth:** Choose how many levels deep to include
  - **Slide layout:** Select how the org is split across slides
  - **Card content:** Control which fields appear on each card in the export
- **What's included:** Exports respect your current filters and highlighting. If you've filtered to Engineering and highlighted by location, the export shows exactly that.

## Screenshot / Visual
The Export dialog showing JPEG and PowerPoint options, with the PowerPoint configuration panel expanded (hierarchy depth, layout, card content options).

## Speaker Notes
PowerPoint export is one of the most-used features for stakeholder communication. The key is that it creates actual PowerPoint slides — not just images. This means you can open them in PowerPoint and make adjustments, add annotations, or integrate them into an existing deck. The configuration options give you control: if you only need the top 3 levels of Engineering for an executive meeting, set the scope and depth accordingly. Tip: apply your filters and highlights before exporting — the export captures exactly what you see on screen, so set up the view for your audience first.
