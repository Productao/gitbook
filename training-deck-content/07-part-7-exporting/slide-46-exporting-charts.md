# Slide 46 — Exporting Charts from Workforce Hub

## Title
Exporting Charts from Workforce Hub

## Lead-line
Turn analytics into presentation-ready visuals — export individual charts or build multi-chart slide packs.

## Key Details
- **Individual chart export:** Open any chart in Workforce Hub → click **Export** → choose your format:
  - **PNG** — Static image. Best for documents, emails, and embedding in reports.
  - **PowerPoint** — Editable chart object. Best for presentations where you want to customize colors, labels, or layout.
  - **CSV** — Raw data behind the chart. Best for custom analysis in Excel or building your own visualizations.
- **Slide Packs (multi-chart export):** Build a custom presentation with multiple charts on different slides → export the entire pack as a **.pptx** file. (See Appendix A6 for details on building Slide Packs.)
- **Filters apply to exports:** Whatever filters you've applied to the chart carry through to the export. Configure the view for your audience before exporting.

## Speaker Notes
Workforce Hub exports are how you make the case for your plan with data. A Layers & Spans of Control chart showing before-and-after is more compelling than a paragraph of text. When presenting to leadership, use the PowerPoint export — it creates editable chart objects, not flat images. This means you can adjust fonts, colors, and labels to match your company's presentation template. Slide Packs are great when you need a multi-chart story — for example, "Slide 1: Headcount by Department, Slide 2: SOC Distribution, Slide 3: Headcount Heatmap by Layer and Department." One export, one .pptx file, ready to present.
