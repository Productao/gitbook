# Slide A6 — Workforce Hub: Slide Packs

## Title
Slide Packs

## Lead-line
Build multi-chart presentations directly in Agentnoon and export them as a ready-to-present PowerPoint deck.

## What Is It?
Slide Packs let you assemble multiple Workforce Hub charts into a single presentation. Each slide in the pack can have a different chart type with its own configuration and filters. The entire pack exports as a .pptx file.

## How to Set It Up
1. Go to **Workforce Hub**
2. Click **"Go to Slide Pack"** (or search for an existing pack)
3. Create a **blank pack** or start from a **template**

## How to Use It
1. **Add slides** → choose a chart type for each slide (Layers & Spans, Headcount Distribution, Heatmap, etc.)
2. **Configure each slide:** Set the chart axes, filters, and display options (counts vs. percentages, sort order)
3. **Title each slide** with a clear label that tells the story
4. **Filters are memorized** across sessions — your configuration persists when you come back
5. **Lock slides** to prevent other collaborators from editing specific slides
6. **Share the pack** with collaborators who have Workforce Hub access
7. **Export as PowerPoint** (.pptx) → ready for presentation

## Speaker Notes
Slide Packs save you the tedious work of exporting charts one by one and assembling them into a deck. Build your recurring monthly or quarterly analytics pack once, and it's ready to export with fresh data every cycle. The lock feature is useful for collaboration — if you and a colleague are building a pack together, lock the slides you've finalized so they don't get accidentally modified. For executive presentations, you can combine Slide Pack exports with org chart PowerPoint exports for a comprehensive deck.
