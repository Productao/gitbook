# Slide A10 — Forecast: Multi-Year Planning

## Title
Multi-Year Planning

## Lead-line
Project workforce costs and headcount up to 5 years into the future for strategic planning and board presentations.

## What Is It?
Multi-Year Planning uses the Forecast module with the Yearly time period to project headcount and cost across a 5-year horizon. Combined with multiple scenario variants, it provides a range of long-term workforce projections for strategic decision-making.

## How to Set It Up
1. Create scenarios with hire dates and effective dates spanning **multiple years** (2026, 2027, 2028, etc.)
2. Set the Forecast **Time Period to Yearly**
3. For comprehensive planning, create **3 scenario variants:**
   - **Conservative** — Minimal growth, lower hiring, higher attrition assumptions
   - **Moderate** — Expected growth based on current trajectory
   - **Aggressive** — Maximum growth, full hiring plan, optimistic retention

## How to Use It
1. Open a scenario → switch to **Forecast**
2. Set **Time Period: Yearly** to see the 5-year view
3. Toggle between **Headcount** and **Cost** to see both dimensions
4. Group by **Department, Location, or any custom field** to see where growth is planned
5. Use **scenario comparisons** to present Conservative vs. Moderate vs. Aggressive side by side
6. **Export** yearly projections for board decks and strategic planning documents

## Planning Approach
- **Year 1:** Detailed (specific positions, exact hire dates, confirmed salaries)
- **Year 2:** Moderate detail (planned roles, estimated timing, rate-card salaries)
- **Year 3+:** Directional (growth targets, estimated headcount, projected cost ranges)
- Present **ranges** rather than exact numbers for years 2+ — acknowledge uncertainty

## Screenshot / Visual
A 5-year Forecast view showing yearly headcount and cost projections, ideally with a growth trajectory visible.

## Speaker Notes
Multi-year planning is most commonly used for annual board presentations and strategic workforce plans. The key is to be realistic about precision — Year 1 should be specific, but Years 3–5 are directional at best. Creating three scenario variants (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive) gives leadership a range instead of a single number, which is more honest and more useful for decision-making. Export the yearly projections and layer them into your existing strategic planning templates.
