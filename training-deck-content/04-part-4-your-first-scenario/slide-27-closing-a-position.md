# Slide 27 — Closing a Position

## Title
Closing a Position

## Lead-line
Model reductions and exits by closing positions — the cost savings appear instantly in your impact tracking.

## Key Details
- **How:** Click on a card → select **"Close Position"** from the action menu
- **Choose a reason:**
  - **RIF (Reduction in Force)** — The position is being eliminated as part of a layoff or headcount reduction
  - **Exit** — The employee is departing voluntarily and the position will not be backfilled
- **Set a termination date (optional):** If the reduction happens at a future date, set the termination date. This phases the change in Forecast — the position exists until that date, then disappears.
- **Visual indicator:** Closed positions appear in **red**, making reductions immediately visible across the org chart
- **Cost impact:** The salary and associated costs are subtracted from your scenario's total, reflected in the OpEx Panel as savings

## Screenshot / Visual
The close position menu showing RIF and Exit options, with the resulting red card visible in the org chart.

## Speaker Notes
Closing positions is how you model headcount reductions. The distinction between RIF and Exit matters for tracking — RIF indicates an involuntary reduction (the organization is eliminating the role), while Exit indicates a voluntary departure where you're choosing not to backfill. In both cases, the position turns red and the cost savings flow through to your OpEx Panel. If you accidentally close the wrong position, use the Activity Log to undo the action. Always set a termination date if the reduction isn't immediate — this ensures your Forecast shows the timing accurately.
