# Slide 23 — Scenario Interface Orientation

## Title
Scenario Interface Orientation

## Lead-line
It looks like Main Org — but now everything is editable, and you have new tools at your disposal.

## Key Details
- **Top bar:** Scenario name displayed in the top-left. Click the dropdown to switch between scenarios. The module switcher lets you jump to Org Chart, Directory, Forecast, or Workforce Hub — all within this scenario's context.
- **Toolbar (same as Main Org + more):** Search, Filter, Highlight, Card Content, Spotlight, Views — plus scenario-only tools like Activity Log, Share, Export, and Comments.
- **OpEx Panel:** The side panel that tracks every change you make — additions, reductions, modifications — with real-time cost and headcount impact. This is new in scenarios.
- **Before / After / Changes toggle:** Switch between viewing your baseline (before), your planned state (after), or just the differences (changes). This is essential for reviewing what you've done.

## Speaker Notes
Take a moment to orient yourself. The scenario interface is intentionally similar to Main Org so there's no learning curve for navigation. The key additions are the OpEx Panel on the side — which we'll cover in detail later — and the Before/After/Changes toggle. That toggle is your best friend: "Show Before" shows what things looked like when you started, "Show After" shows your current planned state, and "Show Changes" isolates only the positions you've modified. Use "Show Changes" frequently to make sure you haven't accidentally changed something you didn't intend to.
