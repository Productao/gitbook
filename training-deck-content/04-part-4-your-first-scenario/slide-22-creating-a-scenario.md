# Slide 22 — Creating a Scenario

## Title
Creating a Scenario

## Lead-line
Three types, one clear naming convention — here's how to create your first scenario.

## Key Details
- **Step 1:** Go to the Homepage → click **"+ Create Scenario"**
- **Step 2:** Choose a scenario type:
  - **Partial Org** — A copy of a specific department or team. Most common and fastest to create. Use this for team-level planning.
  - **Full Org** — A copy of your entire organization. Use for company-wide planning like annual headcount reviews.
  - **New Org** — A blank canvas. Use when building a brand new department or team from scratch.
- **Step 3:** Name it clearly using the pattern: **[Area] [Purpose] [Time Period]** — e.g., "Engineering Q2 2026 Reorg" or "Sales Hiring Plan H2"
- **Step 4 (optional):** Set a budget target, effective date, and description to give your scenario context

## Speaker Notes
Partial Org is the most common choice. It loads faster because it only includes the part of the org you're working with, and it keeps your scenario focused. For example, if you're an Engineering lead planning your Q2 headcount, create a Partial Org scenario with just your department. The naming convention is important — when you have 20 scenarios on the homepage, clear names save everyone time. Include what area it covers, what you're doing, and when. Avoid generic names like "Test" or "Scenario 1."
