# Slide 32 — Scenario Walkthrough: Recap

## Title
Scenario Walkthrough: Recap

## Lead-line
You've learned to create a scenario, make every type of change, and phase your plan over time — here's the summary.

## Key Details
- **Create** a scenario — Choose Partial Org, Full Org, or New Org. Name it clearly.
- **Add** positions — Hover + click the **+** button. Set hire dates for future roles. (Green)
- **Edit** positions — Click to open details. Change title, salary, department, etc. (Blue)
- **Move** positions — Drag and drop, or use Change Manager for entire teams. (Purple)
- **Close** positions — Select RIF or Exit. Set termination dates. (Red)
- **Assign / Detach** people — Move employees between positions. Use the Bench for staging.
- **Bulk operations** — Select multiple positions for batch changes at scale.
- **Set effective dates** — Phase every change with hire dates, termination dates, and effective dates.
- **Next up:** Reviewing the impact of your changes with OpEx Panel, Forecast, and Workforce Hub.

## Speaker Notes
You now have all the building blocks for scenario planning. In practice, most scenarios involve a combination of these actions — you might add some roles, move a team, close some redundant positions, and adjust salaries. Every one of these actions is tracked with a color code and reflected in the OpEx Panel. The next section covers how to review the impact of everything you've done — this is the step where you validate your plan before sharing it with anyone.
