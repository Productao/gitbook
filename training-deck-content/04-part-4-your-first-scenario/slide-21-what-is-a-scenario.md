# Slide 21 — What is a Scenario?

## Title
What is a Scenario?

## Lead-line
A scenario is your sandbox — an editable copy of your org where you can model any change without affecting live data.

## Key Details
- **Safe experimentation:** A scenario is a complete copy of your organization (or a part of it) that you can edit freely. Nothing you do here touches Main Org or your HRIS.
- **What you can do:** Add new positions, edit role details, move teams to new managers, close positions, assign or detach employees — all with real-time cost and headcount tracking.
- **Unlimited versions:** Create as many scenarios as you need. Model a conservative plan and an aggressive one. Try Option A and Option B. Compare them side-by-side.
- **When to create one:** Anytime you need to model a change — reorganization, hiring plan, budget cuts, team restructure, M&A integration, quarterly planning, or just exploring an idea.

## Screenshot / Visual
Homepage showing a list of scenarios with descriptive names (e.g., "Engineering Q2 Reorg," "FY26 Hiring Plan — Aggressive," "Sales Team Restructure").

## Speaker Notes
If Main Org is a photograph of your organization, a scenario is a whiteboard where you can sketch the future. You start with the current picture, then move things around, add new roles, remove redundancies — whatever you need. The critical thing is that it's completely safe. You can be as ambitious or experimental as you want. If you don't like the result, just archive the scenario and start fresh. Every change you make is tracked and costed, so you always know exactly what you're proposing.
