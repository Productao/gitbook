# Slide 24 — Adding a Position

## Title
Adding a Position

## Lead-line
Your first edit — hover over a card, click the + button, and create a new role in seconds.

## Key Details
- **How:** Hover over any card in the org chart → click the **+** button that appears → a new position is created as a direct report to that card
- **Fill in details:** A panel opens where you enter the job title, department, salary, location, hire date, and any other relevant fields. Fill in as much as you can.
- **Visual indicator:** The new position appears in **green**, indicating it's an addition. This color-coding makes it easy to spot new roles across the org chart.
- **Set a hire date:** If this is a planned future hire, set the hire date now. This controls when the position appears in Forecast and phases your headcount plan correctly.
- **Tracked automatically:** The addition immediately shows up in the OpEx Panel with its cost and headcount impact.

## Speaker Notes
Adding a position is the most common action in scenarios. When you click the + button, you're creating a new role that reports to the card you hovered over. The green color immediately tells everyone looking at this scenario "this position is new — it didn't exist in the baseline." Always fill in the hire date if you know when this role should start — this is critical for Forecast accuracy. If you don't set a hire date, the position will appear as if it exists immediately, which may overcount your current headcount.
