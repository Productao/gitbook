# Slide 26 — Moving a Position (Drag & Drop)

## Title
Moving a Position

## Lead-line
Drag a card to a new manager, or move an entire team with one action — this is how you restructure.

## Key Details
- **Move an individual:** Drag any card and drop it on the card of the new manager. The reporting line updates instantly. The moved position appears with a **purple** indicator.
- **Move a team:** Click on a manager's card → click the **three-dot menu (⋮)** → select **"Change Manager"** → choose the new manager. The entire team (all direct and indirect reports) moves together.
- **What changes:** The reporting relationship updates. The position now reports to its new manager. All other attributes (title, salary, department) stay the same unless you edit them separately.
- **Common pattern:** After moving a team, you'll often want to bulk-edit the department field to match the new area. We'll cover bulk operations in a few slides.

## Speaker Notes
This is the core mechanic for reorganizations. Drag and drop works intuitively — grab a card, hover over the new manager, and release. For moving entire teams, the "Change Manager" option is much faster than moving individuals one by one. When you move a position, it's tracked as a "move" (purple) rather than an "edit" (blue) — this distinction helps reviewers understand what changed structurally versus what was a data edit. Note that moving a position doesn't automatically change its department — you'll need to update that separately if the department is changing too.
