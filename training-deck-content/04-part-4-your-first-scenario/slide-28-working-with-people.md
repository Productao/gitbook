# Slide 28 — Working with People (Assign, Detach, Bench)

## Title
Working with People

## Lead-line
Assign employees to positions, detach them for restructuring, or move them to the Bench — positions and people are managed separately.

## Key Details
- **Assign an employee:** Click a vacant position → **Assign Employee** → select from the employee list. The employee's data (name, salary, start date) attaches to the position.
- **Detach an employee:** Click an occupied position → **Detach Employee**. The position remains in the org chart (now vacant), and the employee moves to the **Bench**.
- **The Bench:** A holding area for unassigned employees. Use it during restructuring when you need to temporarily remove people from their current roles before placing them in new ones.
- **Internal transfer pattern:** Detach an employee from their old position → Assign them to a new position. The salary follows the person.
- **Key concept:** Employee salary travels with the person, not the position. When you move someone between roles, their compensation comes along.

## Speaker Notes
This is where the position-vs-people distinction becomes really practical. When you're modeling a reorg, you often need to shuffle people between roles. The pattern is: detach from old position, assign to new position. The old position stays in the org chart as a vacancy (which you might close or backfill), and the person moves to their new role. The Bench is a useful staging area — if you're reorganizing a team and need to figure out where everyone goes, detach them all to the Bench first, then assign them to their new positions one by one. This keeps things clean and avoids accidentally leaving someone in the wrong place.
