# Slide 30 — Understanding Change Indicators

## Title
Understanding Change Indicators

## Lead-line
Every change type has a color — here's how to read the visual language of scenarios.

## Key Details
- **Green** = Addition — A brand new position that didn't exist in the baseline
- **Red** = Reduction — A position that has been closed (RIF or Exit)
- **Blue** = Modification — An existing position where data was changed (title, salary, department, etc.)
- **Purple** = Move — A position that was moved to a different manager or area
- **Orange** = Exit — In some views, a voluntary departure is distinguished from a RIF
- **White / No color** = Unchanged — The position exists in your scenario but hasn't been modified
- **Before / After / Changes toggle:** Use "Show Changes" to filter the view to only positions with color indicators — this isolates your modifications from the unchanged baseline

## Screenshot / Visual
An org chart section showing all change types at once — a green card (new hire), a red card (RIF), a blue card (edit), a purple card (moved), and white cards (unchanged). Include a legend.

## Speaker Notes
These colors are consistent everywhere in Agentnoon — on the org chart, in the Directory (row highlighting), in the OpEx Panel, and in exports. Once you learn the color code, you can scan any scenario and immediately understand what changed. The "Show Changes" toggle is particularly valuable when you're reviewing before submission — it strips away all the unchanged positions and shows only what you've touched. This is what your approvers will focus on, so it's worth reviewing in this mode to make sure everything looks right.
