# Slide 25 — Editing a Position

## Title
Editing a Position

## Lead-line
Click any card to open its details — change the title, salary, department, or any other field.

## Key Details
- **How:** Click on any card in the org chart → the detail panel opens on the side showing all fields organized into groups
- **What you can change:** Job title, department, salary, location, pay grade, employee type, and any custom field your admin has configured. Fields are organized into logical groups (e.g., Job Details, Compensation, Contact Info).
- **Save and see:** Click Save — the card updates immediately. If a field displayed on the card changed, you'll see it reflected right away.
- **Visual indicator:** Edited positions appear with a **blue** indicator, signaling a data modification (as opposed to an addition or reduction).
- **Everything is tracked:** Every edit is logged in the OpEx Panel. If you change a salary from $100K to $120K, the $20K increase shows up in the cost impact.

## Speaker Notes
Editing is straightforward — click the card, change what you need, save. The fields you see depend on what your admin has configured. Some fields may be read-only if they come from your HRIS and aren't editable in Agentnoon. One thing to note: if you change a field like Department, this counts as a modification and shows up in the OpEx Panel. This is important because reviewers and approvers will see every change you've made. If you change salary, the cost impact is calculated automatically.
