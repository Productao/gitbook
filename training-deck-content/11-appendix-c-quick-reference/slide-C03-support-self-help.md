# Slide C3 — Support & Self-Help

## Title
Support & Self-Help

## Lead-line
How to troubleshoot issues on your own and where to get help when you need it.

## Key Details

### Quick Fixes (Try These First)
1. **Refresh the page** — Solves most display and loading issues
2. **Clear browser cache** — Fixes stale data and rendering problems
3. **Try Chrome** — Agentnoon is optimized for Chrome; other browsers may have quirks
4. **Log out and back in** — Resets your session and refreshes permissions
5. **Try incognito/private mode** — Rules out browser extension conflicts

### Documentation Resources
- **Help Center** — Searchable documentation organized by module. Start here for how-to guides, FAQs, and troubleshooting.
- **Video Tutorials** — ~30 minutes for new users (Navigation, Org Chart, Scenarios, Workforce Hub). ~60 minutes for admins (Data Import, Access Control, Field Management).
- **FAQ Section** — Quick answers to the most common questions, organized by topic.
- **Troubleshooting Guides** — Step-by-step diagnostic guides for Login & Access, Data, Scenarios, Performance, and Export issues.

### Contact Support
- **Email:** SupportSWP@dayforce.com
- **Include in your request:**
  - Your organization name
  - What happened (steps to reproduce)
  - What you expected vs. what you saw
  - Screenshots (as many as possible)
  - Your browser and operating system
- **Tip:** Contact your organization's Agentnoon admin first — they can often resolve access, data, and configuration issues without contacting external support.

## Speaker Notes
Most issues can be resolved with the five quick fixes listed here. If you're still stuck, the Help Center documentation is comprehensive — use the search bar and you'll likely find a guide that walks through your specific issue. When you do contact support, the more detail you include upfront, the faster they can help. Screenshots are worth a thousand words. And don't forget your admin — many common issues (access problems, missing data, field visibility) are things your admin can fix directly in the platform without waiting for external support.
