# Slide 39 — Sharing a Scenario

## Title
Sharing a Scenario

## Lead-line
Get the right people involved — share your scenario with view-only, comment-only, or full edit access.

## Key Details
- **How:** Click the **Share** button in the left panel → enter email addresses → choose an access level for each person
- **Three access levels:**
  - **View Only** — Can see the scenario, org chart, changes, and OpEx Panel. Cannot make edits. Best for leadership and stakeholders who need visibility without risk of accidental changes.
  - **Comment Only** — Everything in View Only, plus the ability to leave comments and feedback. Best for reviewers and approvers who want to provide input without editing.
  - **Edit Access** — Everything in Comment Only, plus the ability to make changes (add, edit, move, close positions). Best for collaborative planning when multiple people are building the scenario together.
- **Permissions are respected:** Users can only see data within their existing access group scope. Sharing a scenario doesn't override data access restrictions.
- **Manage access:** The scenario owner can change access levels or remove access at any time.

## Screenshot / Visual
The Share dialog with email fields and the access level selector (View Only / Comment Only / Edit Access).

## Speaker Notes
Sharing is how you bring people into your planning process. A typical pattern: build the scenario with Edit Access for your co-planners, share with Comment Only for your HRBP or Finance partner to review, and share View Only with the executive who'll ultimately approve it. Important: sharing a scenario doesn't give people access to data they normally can't see. If someone has an access group that only shows the Engineering department, they'll only see Engineering positions in the scenario — even if the scenario contains the whole org. This keeps sensitive data protected.
