# Slide 43 — The Approval Lifecycle

## Title
The Approval Lifecycle

## Lead-line
From draft to approved — understand the states your scenario moves through and what happens at each stage.

## Key Details
- **Draft** — The scenario is being built. Fully editable. This is where you do all your planning work.
- **Submitted** — Waiting for approval. The scenario is locked — no one can make edits. Approvers are notified and begin their review.
- **Approved** — All required approvers have signed off. The scenario remains locked. It can now be implemented in your HRIS and optionally merged to Main Org (admin only).
- **Rejected** — An approver has returned the scenario with feedback. The scenario unlocks and you can make changes. Once revised, resubmit with updated justification.
- **Un-submitted (admin action)** — An admin can pull back a submitted scenario if changes are needed before approval completes. The scenario unlocks for editing.
- **After approval:** Implement the changes in your HRIS externally → refresh Main Org data → archive the scenario for historical reference.

## Speaker Notes
The approval lifecycle is linear but has built-in flexibility. Most scenarios flow from Draft to Submitted to Approved. If an approver rejects, the scenario returns to Draft and you can revise — importantly, the rejection reason and all prior comments are preserved so you know exactly what to fix. If something urgent comes up after submission, an admin can un-submit the scenario to unlock it for editing. After approval, the changes are implemented in your actual HRIS (this happens outside Agentnoon), and then the Main Org data is refreshed to reflect the new reality. At that point, the scenario has served its purpose and should be archived.
