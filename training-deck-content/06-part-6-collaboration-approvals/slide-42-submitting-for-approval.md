# Slide 42 — Submitting for Approval

## Title
Submitting for Approval

## Lead-line
When your scenario is ready, submit it through the OpEx Panel — add your justification and let the approval workflow take over.

## Key Details
- **Pre-submission checklist:**
  - Review all changes in "Show Changes" mode — make sure nothing was changed accidentally
  - Verify cost and headcount impact in the OpEx Panel match your targets
  - Check Forecast for correct phasing and effective dates
  - Validate structural health in Workforce Hub
- **How to submit:** Go to the OpEx Panel → click **"Submit"** → add a **justification** explaining your changes and rationale
- **What happens next:** The scenario enters the approval workflow. Approvers at each level are notified. They can review the full scenario — org chart, OpEx Panel, Forecast, and all changes.
- **Approval levels:** Level 0 (optional, scenario-specific) → Level 1 (global approvers, e.g., HRBPs) → Level 2 (global approvers, e.g., CFO) → Level 3 (optional, scenario-specific)
- **Once submitted:** The scenario locks — no further edits until it's approved, rejected, or un-submitted by an admin

## Speaker Notes
The justification is your chance to tell the story of your plan. Don't just write "headcount changes" — explain the why. "This scenario models a Q2 reorg to reduce management compression in Engineering. We're consolidating 3 manager roles into 1, adding 5 senior ICs, and moving the QA team under the VP of Product. Net impact: +2 headcount, -$120K annual cost." A strong justification makes the approver's job easier and increases the chance of a smooth approval. Once you submit, the scenario locks to prevent accidental changes while it's being reviewed.
