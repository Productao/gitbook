# Slide 48 — The Agentnoon Workflow (Recap)

## Title
The Agentnoon Workflow

## Lead-line
Seven steps from understanding your current state to communicating your plan — here's the complete workflow.

## Key Details
- **1. Explore** — Navigate Main Org to understand your current structure, reporting lines, and team composition
- **2. Analyze** — Use Workforce Hub to identify opportunities — span of control issues, headcount imbalances, structural inefficiencies
- **3. Plan** — Create a scenario and model your proposed changes — add, edit, move, and close positions
- **4. Review** — Check impact using the OpEx Panel (cost), Forecast (timing), and Workforce Hub (structural health)
- **5. Collaborate** — Share the scenario, gather feedback via comments, and refine with your team
- **6. Approve** — Submit for approval with a clear justification. Approvers review and sign off.
- **7. Communicate** — Export data, charts, comparisons, and reports for stakeholders and implementation teams

## Screenshot / Visual
A workflow diagram showing all 7 steps in a circular or linear flow, with module icons for each step. This is the "hero graphic" for the deck — it should be visually memorable.

## Speaker Notes
This is the end-to-end workflow that every user should know. In practice, you'll cycle through steps 3–5 multiple times as you build, review, adjust, and refine your plan. The key insight is that Agentnoon isn't just a planning tool — it's a communication tool. The exports and comparisons you create in step 7 are how you bring stakeholders along on the journey. When a plan is well-documented with clear impact analysis and comparisons, it gets approved faster and implemented with more confidence.
