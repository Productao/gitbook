# Slide 50 — Getting Help & Next Steps

## Title
Getting Help & Next Steps

## Lead-line
Here's where to go when you need help, want to learn more, or get stuck.

## Key Details
- **Help Center** — Your first stop for documentation, guides, FAQs, and troubleshooting. Searchable and organized by topic.
- **Video Tutorials** — A ~30-minute learning path for new users covering navigation, org chart, scenarios, and Workforce Hub. ~60 minutes for admins.
- **Support Email** — SupportSWP@dayforce.com. Include: your organization name, what happened (steps to reproduce), screenshots, and your browser/OS.
- **Quick fixes to try first:** Refresh the page, clear browser cache, try Chrome, log out and back in, try incognito mode.
- **Your admin** — Can resolve access issues, data questions, and configuration changes directly. Reach out to them before contacting support for account-related issues.
- **See Appendix C** for keyboard shortcuts, a glossary of key terms, and detailed self-help guidance.

## Speaker Notes
You're not on your own after this training. The Help Center has detailed documentation for every feature we've covered and more. The video tutorials are great if you're a visual learner — they walk through the same workflows we've covered here but with screen recordings. If you hit a real issue, email support with the details listed here — the more specific you are, the faster they can help. And don't forget your organization's admin — they can often resolve access, data, and configuration issues without involving external support. Now go build something!
