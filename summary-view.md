# Summary View

#### Overview

Get a high-level financial overview of your scenario - broken down by type of change, formula, and new vs. current headcount costs.

## Total Financial Overview

1. **Current Headcount Cost**\
   The total cost of all roles in your current org, before any changes are applied.
2. **Sum of Position Changes**\
   The total cost added or removed from scenario actions like additions, deletions, or salary edits—based on the selected formula (e.g., Salary, Total Employer Cost).
3. **Sum of Placeholder Adjustments**\
   Manually entered adjustments representing savings targets or cost assumptions not tied to specific roles.
4. **New Headcount Cost**\
   The combined total of your current cost, scenario changes, and placeholder adjustments.
5. **Remaining Budget**\
   Displays how much budget is left after all changes, helping you track limits or goals against a defined cap.

## View Scenario Changes by Activity

Changes are grouped into collapsible categories such as:

* Additions – cost impact of new hires or added roles
* RIF, Exits – cost impact of reductions in force or terminations
* Changes – cost impact of modifications to employee compensation
* Moved – cost impact of lateral or vertical shifts

Under each group, you can see which roles were affected, helping you track the scope of your scenario actions.&#x20;

### Filter Impact by Custom Formula

Select any formula from the dropdown - like Salary , Total Employer Cost, Severance - to view the financial impact across your scenario.
