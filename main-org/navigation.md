# Orientation & Navigation

Master the art of navigating your org chart with these essential features. The left toolbar in Main Org provides powerful tools for searching, filtering, highlighting, and customizing your view.

## The Left Toolbar

All navigation features are accessed from the left sidebar. Here's your complete guide to each tool:

---

## 1. Search

Find employees or positions instantly by name or job title.

**How to use:**
1. Click the **Search** icon in the left toolbar
2. Type a name (e.g., "Alice Johnson") or job title (e.g., "VP of Engineering")
3. Select from the results
4. The org chart automatically navigates to that position

**Pro tip:** Search works across your entire scoped organization, even if you're currently viewing a different area.

---

## 2. Filtering

Filter the org chart to show only specific positions based on attributes.

**How to use:**
1. Click the **Filter** icon
2. Select an attribute (Department, Location, Pay Grade, etc.)
3. Choose specific values to include
4. Click **Apply**

**Two filtering modes:**

### Standard Filtering
Shows only positions matching your filter criteria. May break the visual hierarchy.

### Manager Filtering (Recommended)
Preserves the org hierarchy by showing managers even if they don't match the filter.

**Example:** If you filter for "Department = Engineering", Manager Filtering will also show the VP and CEO above them to maintain context.

**Pro tip:** Use Manager Filtering to keep the org chart structure intact while focusing on specific groups.

**To clear filters:**
- Click the Filter icon
- Click "Clear All" or remove individual filters

---

## 3. Tree View Navigation

See your org structure as an indented list, similar to a file folder view.

**How to use:**
1. Click the **Tree View** icon
2. Browse the hierarchical list
3. Click any name to navigate to that position in the org chart

**When to use Tree View:**
- Quickly browse deep hierarchies
- See position titles in list format
- Navigate without expanding/collapsing cards

---

## 4. Highlighting

Color-code the org chart based on any attribute for instant visual insights.

**How to use:**
1. Click the **Highlight** icon
2. Choose an attribute (Department, Work City, Pay Grade, etc.)
3. The org chart colors automatically

**Two highlight options:**

### Fill Color (default)
Fills entire cards with color

### Outline Color
Adds colored borders to cards

**You can use both at once!**
- Fill color for one attribute (e.g., Department)
- Outline color for another (e.g., Location)

**Legend:** Click "Show Legend" to see what each color represents.

**Custom colors:** Admins can customize color schemes in Settings.

---

## 5. Card Content (Display Settings)

Control what information appears on each card.

**How to use:**
1. Click the **Card Content** icon
2. Check/uncheck fields to show/hide
3. Drag fields to reorder
4. Changes apply immediately

**Available fields:**
- Standard fields (Name, Job Title, Department)
- Custom fields configured by your admin
- Calculated fields (marked "FX"):
  - Span of Control
  - Total Organization Size
  - Layer
  - Average SOC

**Pro tip:** Keep cards minimal (3-5 fields) for readability. Add more fields only when you need specific analysis.

---

## 6. Spotlight

Highlight specific positions matching criteria while graying out the rest.

**How to use:**
1. Click the **Spotlight** icon
2. Choose a spotlight rule:
   - **Average Immediate SOC** - Find managers with specific span ranges
   - **Field-specific spotlight** - Highlight positions with specific attribute values
3. Set your criteria (e.g., "SOC 1-2")
4. Click **Apply**

**Spotlighted positions appear in blue. All others are grayed out.**

**Common use cases:**
- Find managers with low span of control (1-2 direct reports)
- Identify high spans (16-25 or more)
- Locate positions by pay grade or level
- Highlight specific locations or departments

**Enterprise customers:** May have access to custom spotlight rules configured for their organization.

---

## 7. Presets (Saved Views)

Save your current filter, highlight, and display settings for quick access later.

**How to use:**
1. Configure your view (filters, highlights, card content)
2. Click the **Presets** icon
3. Click "Save Current View"
4. Name your preset (e.g., "Engineering Team - SOC View")
5. Click **Save**

**To use a preset:**
- Click Presets
- Select a saved view
- All settings apply instantly

**Sharing presets:** Other users can access presets you create (based on their permissions).

**Common presets to create:**
- Department-specific views
- SOC analysis views
- Location-based views
- Custom views for recurring analysis

---

## 8. Exporting

Export org charts and data in multiple formats.

**Org Chart Exports:**

### JPEG
- Screenshot of your current org chart view
- Includes all visible cards and formatting
- Good for emails and documents

### PowerPoint
- Exports **one slide per manager**
- Each slide shows that manager and their direct reports
- Perfect for stakeholder presentations

**Data Exports:**

### CSV
- Raw org data in spreadsheet format
- All positions and attributes
- Great for Excel analysis

### Template Builder (Admin)
- Admins can customize export column structure
- Reorder and select specific fields
- Create standardized export templates

**How to export:**
1. Click the **Export** icon
2. Choose format (JPEG, PowerPoint, or CSV)
3. Download the file

---

## 9. Layout Options

Adjust how the org chart displays to suit your needs.

**Layout Modes:**

### Vertical Mode (Top-Down)
- Traditional org chart style
- Shows up to 3 levels at once
- Ideal for management presentations

### Horizontal Mode (Left-Right)
- Alternative layout
- Good for wide teams
- Saves vertical space

### Compact Mode
- Displays **two columns instead of one**
- Useful for managers with 6-10+ direct reports
- Saves screen space

**Expand/Collapse Levels:**
- Choose how many levels to show (1, 2, or 3)
- Collapse deeper levels to focus on leadership
- Expand all to see full organization

**Zoom Controls:**
- **Fit to screen** - Auto-sizes chart to fit your viewport
- **Zoom in/out** - Use trackpad pinch gesture or mouse wheel
- **Pan** - Click and drag to move around

## Navigation Best Practices

1. **Start with search or filter** - Don't manually browse large orgs; use search and filters to narrow focus
2. **Use Manager Filtering** - Preserves context better than standard filtering
3. **Save presets for recurring analysis** - Don't reconfigure the same view repeatedly
4. **Combine highlight + spotlight** - Highlight by Department, Spotlight by SOC for powerful analysis
5. **Keep card content minimal** - Add fields only when needed for specific analysis
6. **Clear filters between sessions** - Filters carry over to other modules
7. **Use Tree View for deep hierarchies** - Faster than expanding cards one-by-one

## Common Workflows

### Finding a Specific Person
1. Click **Search**
2. Type their name
3. Select from results

### Analyzing a Department
1. Click **Filter** → Select Department
2. Enable **Manager Filtering**
3. Click **Apply**
4. Add **Highlight** by Location or Pay Grade for additional insights

### Identifying Compression
1. Click **Spotlight** → "Average Immediate SOC" → "1-2"
2. Review blue highlighted positions
3. Click positions to explore context

### Preparing a Presentation
1. Filter to relevant area
2. Highlight by relevant attribute
3. Export to PowerPoint
4. Present to stakeholders
