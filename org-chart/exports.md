# Exports

### Overview:

Export your org data in multiple formats - customized exactly how you need it.

### Export Options

* **Org Chart Exports:**
  * **JPEG:** Screenshot of the org chart.
  * **PowerPoint:** Exports **a slide per manager**
* **Data Exports:**
  * **EIB File:** For direct integration into external systems like Workday.
  * **CSV:** Exports raw org data.
  * **Template Builder:** Admins can customize and **reorder columns** for structured exports.

### PowerPoint Exports

Export your org chart directly to PowerPoint with full control over layout, content, and structure. Here's how to customize your exports:

* **Add filters** \
  Refine your hierarchy export by adding filters<br>
* **Select hierarchy depth**\
  Select the number of levels you want to export <br>
* **Choose your layout**\
  This helps you fit larger teams or deeper structures more clearly across slides. Select how you want your org chart to appear
  * Vertical
  * Horizontal
  * Compact<br>
* **Set slide depth**\
  Decide how many layers should be displayed per slide. This prevents overcrowding and ensures clear visuals for managers with large teams.<br>
*   **Choose card content**\
    Select the level of detail for each org chart node:

    Show only job titles

    Include all card content (e.g., Department, Level, Compensation)

    ![](<../.gitbook/assets/image (31).png>)<br>

Once your options are set, click **Dowload** to download the fully customized org chart presentation.
