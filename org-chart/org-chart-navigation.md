# 🗺️ Org Chart Navigation

### Main Org Chart Features

The **left toolbar** in Agentnoon provides powerful tools for **searching, filtering, customizing, exporting, and collaborating** within the org chart. This guide walks through each feature.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 12.40.43 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 1. Search

* Find employees by **name or job title**.
* Click on a result to navigate directly to their position in the org chart.

<div align="left" data-full-width="false"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 5.51.12 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 2. Filtering

* Filter the org chart by **any attribute** (e.g., department, location, level).
* **Manager Filtering** is recommended to **preserve hierarchy**, ensuring teams remain visible.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 5.52.04 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 3. Tree View Navigation

* Displays the **org structure as an indented list**, similar to a folder view.
* Clicking on a name navigates to that employee in the org chart.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 12.49.36 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 4. Highlighting

* **Color-codes the org chart** based on an attribute (e.g., department, job level).
* **Color coding** can be done by filling the card or by using outlines (or both!)
* **Legends toggle** allows you to view what each colour represents
* **Admins can customize colors** via the settings page.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 12.52.24 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 5. Display Settings

* Toggle **on/off** various attributes (e.g., **Job Title, Department, Manager Cost**).
* Includes **calculated values** like spans of control and reporting layers.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 12.54.09 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 6. Spotlighting

* Highlights **specific data points in blue**, graying out the rest of the chart.
* Works like **highlighting**, but focuses attention on a particular **subset of positions**.
* **Enterprise customers** may have access to **custom spotlight rules**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 12.57.10 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 7. Presets (Saved Views)

* Save **filter, display, and highlighting settings** as a **preset** for quick access.
* Create multiple views and switch between them **without resetting filters manually**.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 1.00.33 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 8. Exporting

* **Org Chart Exports:**
  * **JPEG:** Screenshot of the org chart.
  * **PowerPoint:** Exports **a slide per manager**
* **Data Exports:**
  * **CSV:** Exports raw org data.
  * **Template Builder:** Admins can customize and **reorder columns** for structured exports.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 1.02.03 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 9. Layout Options

* Switch between **horizontal, vertical, or compact** org chart layouts:
  * **Vertical Mode**: Up to 3 **levels**, ideal for management presentations.
  * **Compact Mode**: Displays **two columns instead of one**, useful for wide teams (6–10+ direct reports).
* **Expand/Collapse Levels**: Show or hide certain parts of the org chart.
* **Fit to Screen & Zoom**: Adjust size via **trackpad/mouse pinch gestures**.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 1.05.09 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### Scenario-Specific Org Chart Features

Available only when working inside **scenarios**.

#### 10. Activity Log

* Tracks **all recent changes** within the scenario.
* **Undo** specific actions directly from the log.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 1.07.01 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 11. Comments & Collaboration

* Leave **comments on positions** for discussions.
* **Tag users** and collaborate in real time.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 1.09.15 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
