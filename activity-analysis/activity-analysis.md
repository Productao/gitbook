# Activity Analysis

### Overview

This guide explains how to use Agentnoon’s Activity Analysis feature to map business activities, allocate effort, and analyze how time is spent across the organization.

#### 1. Choose a Scenario for Activity Analysis

1. Navigate to your scenario and click on the **org chart icon** next to it, then select **Activity Analysis**

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.59.26 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 2. Mapping Business Processes and Activities

1. Hover over your root node and click the + sign to add Level 1 business processes (e.g Sales, Finance, Product)

&#x20;    &#x20;

<div align="center"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.13.00 AM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

2. Hover over the business process and click on the + sign to add activities under each process (e.g., Offline Sales, and Conferences under Sales).

<p align="center">      <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.14.13 AM.png" alt=""><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.16.08 AM.png" alt=""></p>

#### 3. Assigning Roles and Effort

1. Hover over an acitivity and click on the Positions icon to assign positions to it, with a time percentage allocation for each position.

<p align="center"><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.25.38 AM.png" alt=""><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.20.48 AM.png" alt=""></p>

2. Confirm allocations to track effort distribution.

#### 4. Categorizing Activities

1. Click on the activity box to assign it a category to indicate its business impact for example:

* **Value Add** – Directly contributes to business goals.
* **Non-Value Add** – Not directly beneficial but still requires effort.

<div align="center"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.24.08 AM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 5. Navigating and Expanding Activity Mapping

1. Use the left-hand panel to move between activities and expand activity trees.

<div align="center"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.31.10 AM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

2. Click on any activity to drill down further or edit details.

#### 6. Analyzing Activity Effort Split by Category

1. Open Workforce Hub and find the Activity Split by Category chart to visualize your operating model and activity data by category.

<div align="center"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-07 at 1.33.59 AM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

2. Analyze data through different perspectives:

*   **Effort Split by Activity Level** – Use the filter drop down to select the levels of activity you want to visualize for.



    <div align="left"><figure><img src="https://19874675.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/19874675/Screenshot%202024-05-27%20at%2010-56-14%E2%80%AFPM-png.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
* **FTE Count or Cost Perspective** – Assess effort based on full-time equivalent (FTE) allocation or salary cost. Hover over the bars to see the details of FTE count / cost of that bar by that activity

<p align="center">       <img src="https://19874675.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/19874675/Screenshot%202024-05-27%20at%2010.52.25%20PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2024-05-27 at 10.52.25 PM"> <img src="https://19874675.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/19874675/Screenshot%202024-05-27%20at%2010.52.17%20PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2024-05-27 at 10.52.17 PM"></p>

* **Percentage Distribution** – Visualize workload distribution across functions as Absolute, Total %, or Group %.\
