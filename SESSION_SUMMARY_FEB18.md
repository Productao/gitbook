- Budget adjustable mid-planning

**Approval Workflows:**
- Level 1 & 2: Global approvers (set in Settings)
- Level 0 & 3: Scenario-specific approvers
- Lock scenarios upon submission checkbox
- All changes must be approved (no partial approvals yet)
- Approval history tracked
- Previously approved positions stay approved on resubmission

**Admin Capabilities:**
- User management and access control

- Rate cards require fields to exist first
- Auto mapping for automatic field population
- Metrics configuration

- Colors customization

**Position vs Headcount:**
- Positions = roles (stable, for planning)
- Headcount = people (volatile, for talent management)
- Agentnoon is position-first
- Workforce planning uses positions
