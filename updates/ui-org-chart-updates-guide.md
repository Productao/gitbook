# UI Org Chart Updates Guide

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (43).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 1. Field Segregation: Employee vs. Position

Cards now clearly distinguish between employee-related and position-related fields:

* **Employee-related fields** → highlighted in **white**.
* **Position-related fields** → highlighted in **gray** within the form.

This distinction helps users quickly identify and update the correct information.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.48.14 PM.png" alt="" width="227"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 2. Adding an Employee to a Position

To assign an employee to a position:

1. Click the **“+”** icon to add a new position.
2. Use the **paperclip** "<img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (41).png" alt="" data-size="line">" icon to shortlist or attach a specific employee to that position.

Once assigned, a **green icon** will appear on the card, confirming that you successfully added the entry.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.46.08 PM.png" alt="" width="311"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.47.32 PM.png" alt="" width="305"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 3. Editing Fields

Both employee and position details can be edited directly within the card:

* **Employee fields** remain in **white**.
* **Position fields** may appear in different **color shades**, depending on the filter applied under the highlighting feature.

Any updates are applied dynamically and reflected instantly on the card.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.49.01 PM.png" alt="" width="254"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.49.39 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 4. Manager Updates

Manager relationships can be updated using **drag-and-drop** functionality.\
For example: moving a role from **CEO** to **Junior VP of HR**.

When a manager update is made, an **update icon** appears on the card to indicate the change.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.40.49 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 5. Closing (RIF/Exit) or Reopening a Position

When a position is **closed (RIF/Exit)**, the icon updates automatically to reflect this status.\
To reopen the position **(Undo RIF/Exit)**, simply click the same icon again.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.43.32 PM.png" alt="" width="227"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-09-10 at 12.44.22 PM.png" alt="" width="232"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### 6. Summary of Icons

* **Green icon** → Card added by you.
* **Update icon** → Field change (e.g., manager update).
* **Closed position icon** → Position marked as closed (can be reopened).

**Tip:** Use the icons and color highlights as quick visual cues to manage employee and position data efficiently.
