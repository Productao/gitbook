# Annual Planning Checklist

Comprehensive workflow for fiscal year workforce planning.

**Total estimated time: 20-30 hours spread over 2-3 months**

---

## Q3 of Prior Fiscal Year: Pre-Planning

**Estimated time: 6-8 hours**

Lay the groundwork for annual planning.

### Performance Review (2-3 hours)

1. Review current fiscal year performance:
   - Actual headcount vs. original plan
   - Actual costs vs. budget
   - Hiring velocity by quarter
   - Attrition rates by department
2. Calculate key metrics:
   - Hiring conversion rates
   - Time to fill by role type
   - Cost per hire
   - Revenue per employee
3. Document lessons learned:
   - What went well
   - What missed the mark
   - Surprises or unexpected changes

### Target Setting (2-3 hours)

1. Get strategic direction from leadership:
   - Revenue targets for next fiscal year
   - Headcount targets or ranges
   - Budget constraints
   - Strategic priorities
2. Align with Finance on planning assumptions:
   - Revenue/headcount ratios
   - Cost per employee targets
   - Merit increase budgets (typically 3%)
   - Benefits and overhead rates

### Input Gathering (2 hours)

1. Request bottom-up plans from all department leaders:
   - Hiring needs by quarter
   - Organizational changes planned
   - Capability gaps to address
2. Understand major initiatives requiring headcount
3. Identify constraints (budget freezes, hiring caps, etc.)

---

## Q4 of Prior Fiscal Year: Scenario Development

**Estimated time: 10-15 hours**

Build the comprehensive annual plan.

### Create Annual Scenario (30 minutes)

1. Click "Create Scenario"
2. Name it: "FY[Year] Plan" (e.g., "FY2027 Plan")
3. Choose Full Org to copy entire organization
4. Add detailed description:
   - Planning assumptions
   - Strategic priorities
   - Headcount and budget targets
   - Major initiatives
5. Click Create

### Model Fiscal Year Growth (5-8 hours)

**Add all new positions (3-5 hours):**
1. Work through each department systematically
2. Add positions with:
   - Hire dates distributed by quarter
   - Realistic recruiting timelines (2-3 months typical)
   - Salaries from rate cards
   - Manager assignments
3. Align total additions to headcount targets
4. Phase hiring throughout the year:
   - Q1: 30% of hires
   - Q2: 30% of hires
   - Q3: 25% of hires
   - Q4: 15% of hires (slowest hiring quarter)

**Model compensation increases (1-2 hours):**
1. Apply merit increases (typically 3% annual):
   - Set effective date (usually start of fiscal year)
   - Differentiate by performance if data available
2. Model promotions:
   - Update titles and salaries
   - Set promotion dates
3. Add market adjustments for competitive roles

**Model attrition (1 hour):**
1. Close positions for expected turnover
2. Use historical attrition rates by department
3. Spread terminations throughout year
4. Add backfill positions where needed

### Create Scenario Variants (2-3 hours)

Build alternative scenarios to show options:

**Conservative Growth (1 hour):**
1. Duplicate main scenario
2. Rename: "FY[Year] Plan - Conservative"
3. Reduce new hires by 15-20%
4. Focus on critical roles only
5. Push some hiring to later quarters

**Aggressive Growth (1 hour):**
1. Duplicate main scenario
2. Rename: "FY[Year] Plan - Aggressive"
3. Increase new hires by 15-20%
4. Add stretch goals and growth bets
5. Pull some hiring to earlier quarters

**Document differences:**
- Headcount delta between scenarios
- Cost delta between scenarios
- Risk and capability trade-offs

### Analysis & Refinement (2-3 hours)

**Forecast Analysis (1-1.5 hours):**
1. Switch to Forecast view
2. Set to Yearly view, 3-5 years forward
3. Review by department:
   - Headcount ramp by quarter
   - Cost trajectory
   - Span of control impacts
4. Check for issues:
   - Q4-heavy hiring (rebalance to earlier quarters)
   - Managers with too many/few reports
   - Budget overspend or underspend
5. Export forecast data for presentations

**Budget Validation (1 hour):**
1. Open OpEx Panel to see total cost
2. Compare to budget targets
3. Adjust if needed:
   - Reduce or delay hires if over budget
   - Add positions if under budget
4. Get Finance sign-off on numbers

**Stakeholder Review (30 minutes):**
1. Share scenarios with department leaders
2. Validate feasibility of hiring timelines
3. Confirm salary levels are competitive
4. Make final adjustments

---

## Q4 / Q1: Approval & Finalization

**Estimated time: 4-6 hours**

Present, approve, and communicate the plan.

### Executive Presentations (2-3 hours)

**Prepare materials (1 hour):**
1. Export forecast data and create visuals:
   - Headcount by quarter (stacked by department)
   - Cost by quarter (stacked by department)
   - Headcount growth rate
   - Cost as % of revenue
2. Create scenario comparison chart:
   - Conservative vs. Moderate vs. Aggressive
   - Headcount, cost, and capability trade-offs
3. Document key assumptions and risks

**Present to CFO and Finance (30-45 minutes):**
1. Walk through forecast numbers
2. Explain budget alignment
3. Address questions and concerns
4. Get Finance approval

**Present to CEO and Executive Team (30-45 minutes):**
1. Focus on strategic alignment
2. Show scenario options (Conservative/Moderate/Aggressive)
3. Recommend a specific scenario
4. Discuss risks and contingencies
5. Get executive approval

### Formal Approval & Communication (1-2 hours)

1. Submit final scenario for formal approval via OpEx Panel
2. Track through approval workflow (all levels)
3. Address any feedback or rejections
4. Once approved, communicate plan:
   - Share with department leaders
   - Publish summary to organization
   - Brief recruiting team on hiring timelines
   - Set expectations with Finance on budget

### Set Up Tracking (1 hour)

1. Document the approved plan for reference
2. Set up quarterly review schedule:
   - Q1: Actuals vs. Plan review
   - Q2: Actuals vs. Plan, Mid-year forecast update
   - Q3: Actuals vs. Plan review
   - Q4: Actuals vs. Plan, Begin next year planning
3. Create dashboard or tracking spreadsheet
4. Define reporting cadence (monthly or quarterly)

---

## Throughout Fiscal Year: Monitoring & Adjustments

**Estimated time: 2-3 hours per quarter**

Track progress and make course corrections.

### Quarterly Business Reviews (2-3 hours per quarter)

**Actuals Analysis (1 hour):**
1. Refresh Main Org with latest data
2. Compare actual headcount vs. plan:
   - By department
   - By quarter
   - Cumulative variance
3. Analyze hiring velocity and attrition
4. Review budget spend vs. forecast

**Forecast Update (1 hour):**
1. Create "[Quarter] Forecast Update" scenario
2. Adjust remaining year:
   - Push out delayed hires
   - Add unplanned hires
   - Close positions for attrition
3. Update budget forecast

**Reporting (30-60 minutes):**
1. Export updated forecast data
2. Prepare slides showing:
   - Plan vs. Actuals
   - Revised forecast
   - Variance explanations
   - Recommendations
3. Present to Finance and leadership

### Mid-Year Reforecast (if needed)

If significantly off plan at mid-year:
1. Create "FY[Year] Plan - Mid-Year Update" scenario
2. Rebase forecast from current actuals
3. Adjust second half of year
4. Resubmit for approval if budget impact is significant

## Annual Planning Timeline

Typical timeline for fiscal year planning:

### For Calendar Fiscal Year (Jan-Dec)

**July-August (Q3):**
- Review current year performance
- Set targets for next year
- Gather department inputs

**September-October (Q4):**
- Build FY scenarios (Conservative/Moderate/Aggressive)
- Analyze and refine forecasts
- Get stakeholder feedback

**November (Q4):**
- Present to CFO and Finance
- Present to CEO and Executive Team
- Submit for formal approval

**December (Q4):**
- Finalize and communicate approved plan
- Set up quarterly tracking
- Brief recruiting team

**January (Q1):**
- Implement plan
- Begin hiring
- Track progress

### For Offset Fiscal Year

Adjust timeline based on your fiscal year start:
- Begin 5-6 months before FY start (pre-planning)
- Scenario development 3-4 months before FY start
- Approval 2-3 months before FY start
- Finalization 1 month before FY start

## Tips for Annual Planning

### Best Practices

- **Start early:** Begin in Q3 of prior year
- **Bottom-up + top-down:** Combine department inputs with strategic targets
- **Create variants:** Model Conservative, Moderate, and Aggressive scenarios
- **Phase hiring realistically:** Don't cluster all hires in Q1
- **Include attrition:** Model expected turnover
- **Align with Finance:** Get budget approval before finalizing
- **Document assumptions:** Make planning logic transparent
- **Track progress:** Review quarterly, adjust as needed

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Starting too late (rushed planning leads to poor quality)
- Only using top-down targets (ignoring department needs)
- Overly optimistic hiring timelines (leads to Q4 hiring rush)
- Forgetting about attrition (creates unrealistic end-of-year headcount)
- Not creating scenario variants (limits strategic discussion)
- Setting it and forgetting it (annual plans need quarterly updates)

### Integration with Quarterly Planning

Annual plan provides the framework:
- Quarterly plans execute against annual plan
- Each quarter validates or adjusts annual assumptions
- Use quarterly actuals to improve next year's annual planning
- Allow flexibility for adjustments within overall annual budget

## Role-Specific Responsibilities

### Department Leaders
- Submit detailed hiring plans for your team
- Justify headcount needs based on business objectives
- Validate feasibility of timelines
- Commit to execution once approved

### Workforce Planners (HR/Finance)
- Consolidate all department inputs
- Create enterprise-wide annual scenarios
- Ensure strategic and budget alignment
- Present to executive leadership
- Track progress throughout the year

### Finance
- Provide budget targets and constraints
- Review cost forecasts for accuracy
- Validate alignment with revenue plans
- Approve final budget

### Executive Leadership
- Set strategic direction and priorities
- Approve headcount and budget targets
- Choose scenario (Conservative/Moderate/Aggressive)
- Review quarterly progress
