# Workforce Planner Checklist

Guide for HR and Finance teams managing enterprise-wide workforce planning, budgets, and strategic initiatives.

---

## Annual Planning Cycle

**Estimated time: 20-30 hours spread over 2-3 months**

Complete workflow for fiscal year workforce planning.

### Pre-Planning Phase (4-6 hours)

**Review & Analysis (2-3 hours)**
1. Review prior year actuals vs. plan
2. Calculate actual hiring velocity and attrition rates
3. Identify gaps and learnings from last year
4. Document planning assumptions for the new year

**Gather Inputs (2-3 hours)**
1. Collect hiring plans from all department leaders
2. Align with revenue targets and business objectives
3. Get budget constraints from Finance
4. Understand strategic priorities from leadership

### Scenario Development (10-15 hours)

**Create Base Plan (2 hours)**
1. Create scenario named "FY[Year] Workforce Plan"
2. Choose Full Org to copy entire organization
3. Add detailed description of planning scope and assumptions
4. Click Create

**Model Growth by Department (5-8 hours)**
1. Add positions by quarter with realistic hire dates
2. Use rate cards for salary consistency
3. Distribute hires throughout the year (avoid clustering)
4. Align total headcount to revenue/headcount ratio targets
5. Check span of control for all managers as teams grow

**Model Cost Increases (2-3 hours)**
1. Apply merit increases (typically 3% annual)
2. Model promotions for high performers
3. Add market adjustments for competitive roles
4. Update salaries with effective dates

**Create Scenario Variants (1-2 hours)**
1. Save main plan as "FY[Year] - Moderate Growth"
2. Duplicate and modify for "FY[Year] - Conservative Growth" (10-20% fewer hires)
3. Duplicate and modify for "FY[Year] - Aggressive Growth" (10-20% more hires)

### Analysis & Refinement (4-6 hours)

**Forecast Analysis (2-3 hours)**
1. Switch to Forecast view (Yearly view, 3-5 years)
2. Review headcount ramp by department
3. Analyze cost trajectory and budget alignment
4. Check quarterly distribution (avoid Q4-heavy hiring)
5. Export forecast data for presentations

**Scenario Comparison (1-2 hours)**
1. Compare Conservative vs. Moderate vs. Aggressive scenarios
2. Analyze trade-offs (cost, capability, risk)
3. Document recommendations
4. Prepare summary for leadership

**Stakeholder Review (1 hour)**
1. Share scenarios with department leaders for validation
2. Get Finance approval on budget alignment
3. Make adjustments based on feedback
4. Update scenario descriptions with final assumptions

### Approval & Implementation (2-3 hours)

1. Prepare executive presentation with forecast visuals
2. Present to CFO and Finance leadership
3. Present to CEO and Executive Team
4. Submit final scenario for formal approval
5. Communicate approved plan to organization
6. Set up quarterly reviews to track progress vs. plan

## Budget Cuts / RIF Planning

**Estimated time: 8-12 hours (sensitive, time-critical)**

Use this workflow for cost reduction initiatives.

### Preparation (2-3 hours)

**Define Requirements (1 hour)**
1. Get target savings from Finance (e.g., "-$5M in workforce costs")
2. Understand timeline and constraints
3. Determine if cuts are permanent or temporary
4. Clarify severance and benefits impact

**Analyze Current State (1-2 hours)**
1. Review current workforce costs by department
2. Identify high-cost areas or duplicative roles
3. Consider strategic priorities (protect growth areas)
4. Consult with department leaders on business impact

### Scenario Creation (3-5 hours)

**Create Reduction Scenario (30 minutes)**
1. Create scenario named "Budget Reduction - [Target]" (e.g., "Budget Reduction - $5M")
2. Choose Full Org
3. Add description with savings target and timeline
4. Click Create

**Identify Positions to Close (2-3 hours)**
1. Use Directory view with filters:
   - Sort by salary (highest to lowest)
   - Filter by department
   - Filter by performance ratings (if available)
2. Select positions strategically:
   - Consider business impact
   - Avoid cutting critical roles
   - Balance cuts across departments
3. Bulk close positions with effective dates
4. Track progress toward savings target in OpEx Panel

**Validate Organizational Impact (1-2 hours)**
1. Check Span of Control after cuts (avoid leaving managers with too few reports)
2. Identify broken reporting relationships
3. Assess critical role retention
4. Review departmental capability after cuts
5. Make adjustments to minimize disruption

### Scenario Comparison (1-2 hours)

Create multiple cut scenarios to compare options:
1. "Option A - Conservative Cuts" (lower savings, lower risk)
2. "Option B - Moderate Cuts" (balanced approach)
3. "Option C - Aggressive Cuts" (higher savings, higher risk)

Compare scenarios:
- Total savings achieved
- Headcount reduction by department
- Impact on key capabilities
- Risk assessment

### Implementation Planning (2 hours)

1. Model implementation timeline with effective dates
2. Consider phased approach (e.g., Month 1: voluntary, Month 2: involuntary)
3. Document severance costs (if tracked separately)
4. Prepare communication plan
5. Submit final scenario for executive approval

## Compensation Planning

**Estimated time: 6-10 hours**

Annual compensation cycle planning.

### Preparation (2 hours)

1. Get total compensation budget from Finance
2. Review rate cards and market data
3. Gather manager recommendations for merit and promotions
4. Understand company compensation philosophy

### Scenario Creation (3-5 hours)

**Create Comp Plan Scenario (15 minutes)**
1. Create scenario: "FY[Year] Compensation Plan"
2. Choose Full Org
3. Add description with total budget and guidelines

**Model Merit Increases (1-2 hours)**
1. Apply merit increases to eligible positions (typically 3% average)
2. Differentiate by performance rating:
   - High performers: 4-5%
   - Solid performers: 2-3%
   - Low performers: 0-1%
3. Set effective date (typically start of fiscal year)

**Model Promotions (1-2 hours)**
1. Update titles for promoted employees
2. Increase salaries to new level (typically 8-15%)
3. Change reporting relationships if becoming managers
4. Set promotion effective dates

**Model Market Adjustments (1 hour)**
1. Identify roles below market rate
2. Add off-cycle increases to close gap
3. Set effective dates

### Analysis & Approval (1-3 hours)

1. Verify total cost aligns with budget
2. Check for equity issues (pay gaps by gender, ethnicity, etc.)
3. Export data for Finance review
4. Submit for approval

## Quarterly Business Reviews

**Estimated time: 3-4 hours per quarter**

Track and report on workforce plan progress.

### Actuals Analysis (1-2 hours)

1. Refresh Main Org with latest HRIS data
2. Compare actual headcount vs. plan
3. Analyze hiring velocity (hires per month)
4. Track attrition (voluntary vs. involuntary)
5. Review budget spend vs. plan

### Forecast Update (1 hour)

1. Create "[Quarter] [Year] Forecast Update" scenario
2. Adjust remaining year based on:
   - Delayed hires (push dates out)
   - Unplanned attrition (close positions)
   - New positions approved mid-year (add positions)
3. Update budget forecast

### Reporting (1 hour)

1. Export forecast data showing:
   - Plan vs. Actuals (headcount and cost)
   - Revised forecast for remainder of year
   - Variance explanations
2. Prepare slides for leadership
3. Recommend course corrections if needed

## Tips for Workforce Planners

### Before You Start

- Complete the [Quick Start Checklist](../quick-start-checklist.md)
- Review [Forecast Overview](../../forecast/overview.md)
- Review [Budget Planning & Tracking](../../forecast/budget-planning-tracking.md)
- Understand your organization's planning process and timelines

### Best Practices

- **Use consistent naming:** Standardize scenario names across cycles
- **Document assumptions:** Always explain your planning logic
- **Create variants:** Model multiple scenarios to show options
- **Version control:** Save major versions before making big changes
- **Export regularly:** Keep copies of forecasts for historical tracking
- **Collaborate with Finance:** Align on budgets before finalizing plans

### Working with Stakeholders

- **Department leaders:** Gather bottom-up inputs, validate feasibility
- **Finance:** Align on budgets, review cost forecasts
- **Leadership:** Present options, explain trade-offs, recommend approach
- **Admins:** Coordinate on data refreshes and access
