# Parts of the Application

Agentnoon is organized into five main modules, each designed for specific workforce planning tasks. Understanding where to go for what you need will help you work efficiently.

## The Five Main Modules

### 1. **Main Org** (View-Only Org Chart)

**What it is:** Your current organizational structure, synced from your HRIS system.

**When to use it:**
- Explore your current organization
- Search for specific people or positions
- Analyze current span of control and org metrics
- View real-time data without making changes

**Key features:**
- Interactive org chart visualization
- Search and filtering
- Spotlight (finding positions matching criteria)
- Card content customization
- Highlighting by attributes
- Directory view with sortable columns

**Important:** Main Org is **view-only**. To model changes, create a Scenario.

---

### 2. **Scenarios** (What-If Planning)

**What it is:** Editable copies of your org where you can model changes without affecting your live data.

**When to use it:**
- Plan a reorganization
- Model budget cuts or expansions
- Test different team structures
- Prepare for annual planning cycles
- Propose changes before implementing

**Key features:**
- Full editing capabilities (add, move, close positions)
- Bulk operations (change manager for multiple positions at once)
- Change tracker (see all modifications and cost impact)
- Time-based planning (schedule changes for future dates)
- Approval workflows
- Scenario comparisons (compare multiple options side-by-side)

**Pro tip:** Create multiple scenarios to compare different approaches before committing to one.

---

### 3. **Forecast** (Multi-Quarter Headcount Planning)

**What it is:** Forward-looking headcount and budget planning tool for projecting future needs.

**When to use it:**
- Build annual hiring plans
- Project headcount growth by quarter
- Plan budget allocation over time
- Model phased hiring (Q1: 5 hires, Q2: 3 hires, etc.)
- Track forecast vs actuals

**Key features:**
- Multi-quarter/multi-year planning
- Budget constraints and tracking
- Headcount projections
- Cost forecasting
- Variance analysis

**Note:** Forecast is for aggregate planning. For specific org structure changes, use Scenarios.

---

### 4. **Workforce Hub** (Analytics & Insights)

**What it is:** Pre-built analytics dashboards providing insights into your organizational structure.

**When to use it:**
- Analyze span of control across the organization
- View headcount distribution by layer, department, or location
- Identify organizational patterns and anomalies
- Generate reports for leadership
- Export charts and data for presentations

**Key features:**
- **Layers and Spans of Control matrix** - Bird's eye view of management structure
- **Headcount Distribution** - Breakdown by layer, department, location, etc.
- **Headcount Heatmap** - Visual distribution across dimensions
- **Span of Control Distribution** - Manager vs IC breakdown
- Downloadable charts (PNG, PowerPoint, CSV)
- Interactive filtering and drill-down

**Pro tip:** Use Workforce Hub to identify opportunities for efficiency, then model solutions in Scenarios.

---

### 5. **Directory** (People & Position Listing)

**What it is:** Searchable, sortable table view of all people and positions in your scope.

**When to use it:**
- Find specific individuals quickly
- Sort and filter by any attribute
- Export lists to Excel/CSV
- View detailed information in table format
- Analyze data that's easier to see in rows and columns

**Key features:**
- Customizable column display
- Advanced filtering
- Multi-sort capability
- Bulk export
- Direct links back to org chart view

---

## The Homepage

When you first log in, you'll land on the **Homepage**, which shows:

- Your recently accessed scenarios
- Quick links to create new scenarios
- Access to documentation (top right)
- Link to contact support
- Connection speed test
- Your current instance name

**Keyboard shortcuts:**
- Press **1** to jump to Main Org
- Press **2** to jump to Scenarios
- Press **3** to jump to Forecast
- Press **4** to jump to Workforce Hub
- Press **5** to jump to Directory

---

## Navigation Flow

Here's how these modules typically work together:

1. **Start in Main Org** to understand current state
2. **Go to Workforce Hub** to analyze and identify opportunities
3. **Create a Scenario** to model potential changes
4. **Use Forecast** to plan future headcount needs
5. **Reference Directory** for detailed data exports

---

## Filters and Settings

**Important:** Filters set in one module often carry over to others. If you're seeing unexpected data:

1. Check the filter icon (usually top-right)
2. Clear any active filters
3. Verify you're viewing the correct scope

---

## Access and Permissions

Depending on your role, you may have access to:
- **All modules** (full access)
- **Specific departments** (scoped access)
- **Read-only** vs **edit access** (scenarios only)

Contact your admin if you need access to additional areas.
