# Talent Selection

### Overview

This guide explains how to separate an employee from a position, reassign employees, and use shortlisting for succession planning in Agentnoon.

### Detaching an Employee from a Position

1. Click **Assign to position** to attach/detach a person from a position.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.25.31 PM.png" alt="" width="168"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. The **position remains intact**, but it is now marked as **vacant**.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.27.09 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Assigning a New Employee to a Position

1. Click **Add to Position** on a vacant role.
2. Select a new employee from your dataset.
3. When an employee is assigned:

* **Their employee-level data (e.g., Name, Salary, Gender)** is **transferred** to the position.
* The position retains its **existing structure and attributes**.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.28.30 PM.png" alt="" width="345"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Shortlisting Multiple Candidates for a Position

1. If a position has **multiple potential candidates**, you can **shortlist them**:
   * Click **Shortlist** and select multiple candidates.
   * When ready, assign one candidate from the shortlist.
   * (For enterprise customers) Define **rationale for selection**—this can be customized during onboarding.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.29.51 PM.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Displaying the Shortlist on the Org Chart

1. To **show all shortlisted candidates** for a position:
   * Go to **Card Content**.
   * Navigate to **Default Fields**.
   * Select **Position Shortlist** to display the list.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.30.44 PM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

By using **talent selection and shortlisting**, you can efficiently manage **vacancies, succession planning, and workforce transitions** in Agentnoon.
