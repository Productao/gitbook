# Scenario Creation

### Overview

This guide explains the three ways to create scenarios in Agentnoon, how to name and track them, and how to navigate between different scenarios.

### Ways to Create a Scenario

1. **Select Everyone**: Duplicates the entire org chart into a new scenario.
2. **Blank Scenario**: Creates an **empty canvas** for workforce planning.
3. **Filtered Selection**: Allows scenario creation for a specific subset of employees using filters.

### Steps to Create a Scenario

1. Locate the **+** icon in the bottom left of your home screen <img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-05-07 at 9.56.14 PM.png" alt="" data-size="line">
2. You can also pen your **Main Org Chart** → Click **Create Scenario**.
3. Select one of the three methods above.
4. Enter a **unique scenario name**.
5. (Optional) Set a **budget** to track financial impact.
6. Click **Next** to generate the scenario.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 9.55.33 AM.png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

### Navigating Scenarios

1. The **scenario name** appears in the **top left corner**.
2. Click the **dropdown menu** to switch between different scenarios.
3. To return to your **baseline (original data)**, select the **first scenario** in the dropdown, marked with a **view button**.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-03-06 at 9.56.33 AM.png" alt="" width="373"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

By using scenarios, you can **model organizational changes, test different workforce structures, and analyze potential impacts** in Agentnoon.
