# User Collaboration

### Overview

This guide explains how to collaborate within scenarios in Agentnoon using **scenario sharing** and **comments** for real-time discussions and planning.

#### 1. Sharing a Scenario

1. Click the **Share** button on the left panel.
2. Invite users who have access to your instance.
3. **Access Controls Apply**:
   * If a user has restricted access to specific teams, they will **only see scenario changes** for those teams.
   * Scenario-level access **does not override** existing user permissions.

### 2. Using Comments for Collaboration

#### General Comments

1. Type a comment to **discuss changes** or provide context.
2. Engage in **conversations within the scenario** by responding to comments.

#### Position-Specific Comments

1. Comment directly **on a specific position**.
2. Example: If **Sam Hart** holds a position, you can leave a note like:

* _"Need to plan for him."_
* Tag relevant team members for feedback.

### 3. Live Collaboration

See who’s actively working on the board in real time.\
When collaborators are viewing or making changes, their profile icons appear at the top of the board.\
This helps you stay aware of ongoing edits and coordinate seamlessly with your team.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.38.55 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

By leveraging **scenario sharing, comments and live collaboration**, teams can **collaborate efficiently, track scenario changes, and communicate decisions seamlessly** in Agentnoon.
