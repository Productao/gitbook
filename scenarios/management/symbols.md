# Symbols

### Overview

Scenario symbols visually mark the changes you’ve made to roles or employees - like new additions, exits, or edits - so you can easily track updates, communicate intent, and stay aligned while planning.

### RIF&#x20;

Used to indicate roles that have been marked for reduction in force (RIF) or layoff.

### Exit&#x20;

Represents employees who have resigned or are leaving the organization voluntarily.

### Edit&#x20;

Marks any edits made to an employee or position’s data - ike title, salary, or department.

### Newly Added Positions&#x20;

Indicates a role or employee that has been added as part of the scenario.

### Move&#x20;

Represents a position that has been moved to a different manager or department in the org chart.

### Bench&#x20;

Used for roles you’re unsure about - often to flag positions you’re still evaluating or want to revisit later. No immediate decision is tied to it.

### Detach from Position&#x20;

Indicates that an employee has been unassigned, leaving the position vacant but intact for future reassignment.

### Closed Position&#x20;

Marks a vacant role that has been filled and closed.
