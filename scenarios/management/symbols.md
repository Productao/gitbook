# Symbols

Scenario symbols visually mark the changes you've made to roles or employees — so you can easily track updates, communicate intent, and stay aligned while planning.

## RIF

Indicates a role marked for reduction in force (RIF) or layoff.

## Exit

Represents an employee who has resigned or is leaving the organization voluntarily.

## Edit

Marks any edits made to an employee or position's data — title, salary, or department.

## Newly Added Positions

Indicates a role or employee added as part of the scenario.

## Move

Represents a position moved to a different manager or department in the org chart.

## Bench

Flags positions you're still evaluating or want to revisit. No immediate decision is tied to it.

## Detach from Position

Indicates an employee has been unassigned, leaving the position vacant but intact for future reassignment.

## Closed Position

Marks a vacant role that has been filled and closed.

Scenario symbols visually mark the changes you’ve made to roles or employees - like new additions, exits, or edits - so you can easily track updates, communicate intent, and stay aligned while planning.
