# Single Position Interactions

### Overview

This guide explains the different actions you can perform on a **single position** in Agentnoon, including dragging and dropping, editing, closing, deleting, duplicating, and adding direct reports.

### Position Interactions

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.07.10 PM.png" alt="" width="209"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 1. Drag and Drop

1. Click and **drag an individual position** to a new **manager or reporting structure**.
2. Drag an **entire team** (a position and its direct reports) to **reassign it under a new manager**.
3. This interaction updates the **org structure dynamically** and maintains reporting relationships.

#### 2. Editing a Position

1. Hover over a position and click the **Edit** button.
2. Modify details such as **Job Title, Country, and other attributes**.
3. Save changes using the **Save** button in the sidebar.

#### 3. Closing a Position

1. Click the **Minus (-) button** to mark a position as:
   * **Layoff (Reduction in Force)**
   * **Exit (Voluntary Departure)**
2. Closed positions remain in the org chart and **track financial savings**.

#### 4. Deleting a Position

1. **Permanently removes** a position from the scenario.
2. Not recommended unless **cleaning up** the data.
3. Unlike closing, deletion **does not track savings or historical data**.

#### 5. Duplicating a Position

1. Click the **Duplicate** button to create multiple copies.
2. Options include duplicating **1x, 5x, or a custom number of times**.

#### 6. Adding a Direct Report

1. Click **Add Direct Report** to create a **new subordinate position**.
2. The new position card appears under the **parent role** for further details, and you’ll see a green icon on the bottom-left corner indicating it’s a newly added position.

#### 7. Move Cards Left to Right

1. Click the **Arrow** keys on the card to move it left or right

By using these interactions, you can **effectively modify, restructure, and analyze workforce changes** within Agentnoon.
