# Notifications and Activity Tracking

Stay informed about scenario activity with notifications for @mentions, comments, changes, and collaboration events. Configure your notification preferences to get the right updates at the right time.

---

## Types of Notifications

### @Mention Notifications

**When you receive:**
- Someone @mentions you in a comment
- On general scenario comment
- On position-specific comment

**What you get:**
- Email notification (if enabled)
- In-app notification in Agentnoon inbox
- Badge/count indicator

**How to access:**
1. Click notification bell icon in Agentnoon
2. See list of @mentions
3. Click to go directly to comment

---

### Comment Notifications

**When you receive:**
- New comment on scenario you own
- Reply to your comment
- Comment on position you're watching
- Comment thread you're part of

**What you get:**
- Email notification (if enabled)
- In-app notification
- Comment count badge

**Customize:**
- Turn on/off email notifications for comments
- Choose which comment types trigger notifications
- Set notification frequency (immediate, digest, etc.)

---

### Scenario Change Notifications

**When you receive:**
- Major changes to scenarios you're watching
- Scenario shared with you
- Scenario you're collaborating on is edited
- Scenario submitted for approval (if you're approver)

**What you get:**
- In-app notification
- Optional email digest

---

### Collaboration Activity

**When you receive:**
- New user joins scenario you own
- User starts editing scenario you're in
- Access level changes
- Scenario ownership transfers

**What you get:**
- In-app notification
- Real-time presence indicators

---

## Notification Settings

### Accessing Notification Preferences

1. Click profile icon or settings
2. Select **Notifications** or **Preferences**
3. Configure notification types
4. Choose email frequency
5. Save changes

---

### Configurable Options

**Email notifications:**
- @mentions: On/Off
- Comments: On/Off, Immediate/Digest
- Scenario changes: On/Off
- Sharing events: On/Off

**In-app notifications:**
- Always visible in Agentnoon inbox
- Can't be fully disabled
- Badge indicators show unread count

**Frequency:**
- Immediate (each event)
- Hourly digest
- Daily digest
- Weekly summary

---

## Activity Feed

### Viewing Scenario Activity

**In-app activity feed:**
1. Open scenario
2. Click **Activity** icon (if available)
3. See chronological list of:
   - Position changes
   - Comments
   - User joins/leaves
   - Sharing events
   - Approvals

**What you can see:**
- Who made the change
- What changed
- When it happened
- Link to specific change

---

### Filtering Activity

**Filter by:**
- Activity type (comments, edits, sharing)
- User (show only changes by specific person)
- Date range
- Position or department

**Use cases:**
- See all changes by specific planner
- Review all comments from last week
- Track edits to specific department
- Audit scenario activity

## Managing Notification Overload

### Best Practices

1. **Customize frequency** - Use digest mode for high-activity scenarios
2. **Mute low-priority scenarios** - Turn off notifications for reference scenarios
3. **Enable critical notifications only** - @mentions and approval requests
4. **Check in-app regularly** - Don't rely solely on email
5. **Set expectations with team** - Agree on response times for notifications

---

### Notification Strategies by Role

**Scenario Owner:**
- Enable: @mentions, comments, major changes
- Consider: Daily digest for minor edits
- Critical: Approval notifications

**Collaborator:**
- Enable: @mentions, direct comments
- Optional: All comments, change notifications
- Consider: Muting after your input is complete

**Reviewer:**
- Enable: @mentions, replies to your comments
- Optional: Scenario change notifications
- Consider: Turning off after review is complete

**Observer (View Only):**
- Enable: @mentions only
- Optional: Weekly summary of changes
- Consider: Muting entirely for archived scenarios

## Common Notification Workflows

### Workflow 1: Active Collaboration

**Scenario:** Building scenario with 2 co-planners over 1 week.

**Notification setup:**
- Enable immediate email for @mentions
- Enable daily digest for all comments
- Enable in-app for all changes
- Check in-app feed 2-3 times per day

**Result:** Stay informed without notification overload.

---

### Workflow 2: Stakeholder Review

**Scenario:** 5 stakeholders reviewing scenario, leaving comments.

**Notification setup:**
- Enable immediate email for @mentions
- Enable in-app for all comments
- Check notifications once per day
- Respond to @mentions within 24 hours

**Result:** Responsive to direct questions, informed on feedback.

---

### Workflow 3: Post-Approval Monitoring

**Scenario:** Approved scenario, monitoring for any changes.

**Notification setup:**
- Enable immediate email for any edits (shouldn't happen often)
- Disable comment notifications (scenario is approved)
- Check activity feed weekly

**Result:** Alert to unexpected changes without noise.

---

### Workflow 4: Reference Scenario

**Scenario:** Archived scenario kept for reference, no active work.

**Notification setup:**
- Disable all email notifications
- Keep in-app notifications (low volume expected)
- Check only if you see badge count

**Result:** No noise from inactive scenarios.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Not receiving @mention notifications.
- **Solution:** Check notification settings. Verify email is correct in profile. Check spam folder. Ensure notifications are enabled for @mentions.

**Problem:** Too many notification emails.
- **Solution:** Switch from immediate to digest mode. Disable notifications for less critical scenarios. Mute scenarios after your input is complete.

**Problem:** In-app notification badge not updating.
- **Solution:** Refresh page. Log out and log back in. Check browser permissions. Try different browser.

**Problem:** Don't see activity feed for scenario.
- **Solution:** Feature may not be available in your Agentnoon version. Check with admin. Use Change Tracker as alternative.

**Problem:** Notifications delayed or not arriving in real-time.
- **Solution:** Check notification settings. Email delays may be on email provider side. In-app notifications should be immediate (refresh page).

**Problem:** Want to unsubscribe from scenario notifications.
- **Solution:** Open scenario settings or notification preferences. Mute or disable notifications for that specific scenario. Or click unsubscribe link in notification emails.

**Problem:** Missing notifications for important events.
- **Solution:** Check notification settings - may have been disabled accidentally. Review email filters/rules. Check spam folder. Verify you have required access level to scenario.

## Best Practices Summary

1. **Enable @mentions** - These are usually direct requests for your input
2. **Use digest mode for busy scenarios** - Avoid email overload
3. **Check in-app feed regularly** - Don't rely solely on email
4. **Mute completed scenarios** - Reduce noise from inactive work
5. **Respond to @mentions promptly** - Shows respect for collaborators' time
6. **Configure per scenario** - Different scenarios need different notification levels
7. **Review settings quarterly** - Adjust as your workload changes
8. **Use real-time presence** - Know who's online before expecting immediate response
