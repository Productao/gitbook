# Scenario Comparisons

When you need to choose between different organizational approaches, scenario comparisons let you evaluate multiple options side-by-side. Compare cost, headcount, structure, and more to make data-driven decisions.

## Why Compare Scenarios

**Common reasons to compare:**

- **Evaluate alternatives** - Test different reorganization approaches
- **Choose best budget cut option** - Compare impact of different reduction strategies
- **Assess succession candidates** - Model different people in leadership roles
- **Compare growth strategies** - Evaluate hiring plans with different structures
- **Present options to leadership** - Show stakeholders multiple approaches with data

**Example:** You're planning a reorganization and created three scenarios:
- **Scenario A:** Combine two teams under one VP
- **Scenario B:** Keep teams separate, add a director layer
- **Scenario C:** Flatten structure, remove middle management

Comparison view helps you see cost differences, headcount impact, and structural tradeoffs to choose the best option.

---

## Accessing Scenario Comparisons

### From Homepage

1. Go to your **Agentnoon homepage**
2. You'll see list of your scenarios
3. Select **2-4 scenarios** to compare (click checkboxes)
4. Click **Compare** button (bottom-right corner)
5. Comparison view opens

**Pro tip:** You need at least 2 scenarios to compare. You can compare up to 4 scenarios simultaneously.

---

### From Inside a Scenario

1. Open a scenario
2. Click **Compare** in the taskbar
3. Select additional scenarios to compare
4. Comparison view opens

## The Comparison View

### Overview Layout

**Top Section:**
- Scenario names

- Effective dates (if set)

**Main Canvas:**
- Side-by-side org charts OR

- Switch between comparison modes

## What You Can Compare

### 1. Cost Comparison

**What it shows:**
- Total cost for each scenario
- Cost difference vs Main Org (baseline)
- Cost difference between scenarios
- Visual bar charts

**How to view:**
1. In comparison view, select **Cost** from dropdown
2. See cost breakdown by scenario
3. Compare total compensation cost

**When to use:**
- Budget-constrained planning
- Evaluating financial impact of alternatives
- Presenting cost tradeoffs to finance team

**Example:**
- Scenario A: $12.5M (baseline + $500K)
- Scenario B: $11.8M (baseline - $200K)
- Scenario C: $13.2M (baseline + $1.2M)

**Decision:** Scenario B saves money while Scenario C is most expensive.

---

### 2. Headcount Comparison

**What it shows:**
- Total headcount for each scenario
- Headcount difference vs Main Org
- Headcount difference between scenarios
- Breakdown by department (if available)

**How to view:**
1. Select **Headcount** from dropdown
2. See employee count by scenario
3. Compare additions/reductions

**When to use:**
- Annual hiring planning
- RIF planning
- Growth scenario evaluation

**Example:**
- Scenario A: 850 employees (+50 vs Main Org)
- Scenario B: 800 employees (0 vs Main Org)
- Scenario C: 780 employees (-20 vs Main Org)

**Decision:** Scenario A grows team, Scenario C reduces headcount.

---

### 3. Organizational Structure Comparison

**What it shows:**
- Side-by-side org charts
- Visual representation of different structures
- Reporting relationships
- Team hierarchies

**How to view:**
1. Select **Org Chart** or **Structure** view
2. See scenarios side-by-side
3. Scroll to compare different parts of org

**When to use:**
- Evaluating reorganization approaches
- Comparing reporting structures
- Visualizing team configurations

**What to look for:**
- Span of control differences
- Management layers
- Team sizes
- Reporting clarity

---

### 4. Pyramid Chart Comparison

**What it shows:**
- Organizational hierarchy visualized as pyramid
- Distribution of employees by level
- Management vs individual contributor ratios
- Comparison of organizational depth

**How to view:**
1. Select **Pyramid Chart** from dropdown
2. See stacked visualization of org levels
3. Compare width (headcount at each level) across scenarios

**When to use:**
- Evaluating organizational flatness
- Comparing management layers
- Assessing span of control across scenarios
- Understanding organizational complexity

**What to look for:**
- **Wide pyramid** = Flatter organization, larger teams
- **Tall pyramid** = More layers, smaller teams
- **Top-heavy** = High ratio of managers to ICs
- **Balanced** = Reasonable manager-to-IC ratio

**Example:**
- Scenario A: 5 layers, wide at bottom (IC-heavy)
- Scenario B: 7 layers, narrower (more management layers)
- Scenario C: 4 layers, very wide (flattest)

**Decision:** Scenario C is flattest but may have span of control issues.

---

### 5. Key Metrics Comparison

**What it shows:**
- Summary statistics side-by-side
- Span of control averages
- Layers of management
- Department sizes
- Any other configured metrics

**How to view:**
1. Select **Metrics** view
2. See table or dashboard of key stats
3. Compare numbers across scenarios

**When to use:**
- Data-driven decision making
- Presenting to stakeholders with specific metrics
- Comparing organizational health indicators

## Comparing More Than Two Scenarios

### Add Additional Scenarios

**How to add:**
1. In comparison view, look for **arrow buttons** at bottom
2. Click **+ Add Scenario** or right arrow
3. Select additional scenario (up to 4 total)
4. New column appears in comparison

**How to remove:**
1. Click **X** on scenario column
2. Scenario removed from comparison

**Best practices:**
- Compare 2-3 scenarios for clarity (4 can be overwhelming)
- Name scenarios clearly so comparison is easy to follow
- Use similar effective dates for apples-to-apples comparison

## Interpreting Comparison Results

### Look for Key Differences

**Cost:**
- Which scenario fits budget constraints?
- What's the cost range between options?
- Are savings worth structural tradeoffs?

**Headcount:**
- Which scenario aligns with hiring targets?
- Are additions/reductions realistic?
- Does headcount distribution make sense?

**Structure:**
- Which structure is clearest for employees?
- Are spans of control manageable?
- How many management layers?
- Are reporting relationships logical?

**Complexity:**
- Which scenario is simplest to implement?
- Fewest disruptions to existing teams?
- Easiest to communicate?

## Common Comparison Workflows

### Workflow 1: Evaluating Budget Cut Options

**Goal:** Choose least disruptive way to reduce cost by 10%.

**Steps:**
1. Create **Scenario A:** Close positions in Department X
2. Create **Scenario B:** Close positions across all departments evenly
3. Create **Scenario C:** Flatten management structure
4. Go to homepage → Select all 3 → Click **Compare**
5. Select **Cost** view
6. Verify all achieve ~10% savings
7. Switch to **Org Chart** view
8. Evaluate structural impact of each
9. Switch to **Headcount** view
10. Review which roles are eliminated
11. Choose scenario with best balance of savings and minimal disruption

**Result:** Data-driven decision on budget cut approach.

---

### Workflow 2: Choosing Between Reorganization Structures

**Goal:** Decide between two restructuring approaches.

**Steps:**
1. Create **Scenario A:** Combine teams under one VP
2. Create **Scenario B:** Keep teams separate, add directors
3. Compare both scenarios
4. Select **Org Chart** view - see visual difference
5. Select **Pyramid Chart** - compare layers/flatness
6. Select **Metrics** - compare span of control
7. Select **Cost** - verify neutral cost (just moves)
8. Choose clearer structure

**Result:** Best reorganization approach selected.

---

### Workflow 3: Succession Planning Comparison

**Goal:** Evaluate three candidates for VP role.

**Steps:**
1. Create **Scenario A:** Candidate 1 as VP
2. Create **Scenario B:** Candidate 2 as VP
3. Create **Scenario C:** Candidate 3 as VP
4. Compare all 3 scenarios
5. Select **Org Chart** view
6. Evaluate each candidate's team structure
7. Consider: Who has strongest team? Best span of control? Fewest gaps?
8. Select **Cost** view (if compensation differs)
9. Choose best succession option

**Result:** Informed succession decision.

---

### Workflow 4: Annual Planning Alternatives

**Goal:** Compare three hiring plans for next year.

**Steps:**
1. Create **Scenario A:** Aggressive hiring (100 new positions)
2. Create **Scenario B:** Moderate hiring (50 new positions)
3. Create **Scenario C:** Conservative hiring (25 new positions)
4. Compare all 3
5. Select **Cost** view - see cost implications
6. Select **Headcount** view - see growth trajectories
7. Select **Org Chart** view - see team sizes
8. Present all 3 to leadership with data
9. Get approval for chosen scenario

**Result:** Aligned annual hiring plan.

## Best Practices

1. **Name scenarios descriptively** - "Q2 Reorg Option A" beats "Scenario 1"
2. **Use consistent effective dates** - Compare apples to apples
3. **Compare 2-3 scenarios** - More than 3 gets cluttered
4. **Start with cost/headcount** - Quick summary view first
5. **Then review structure** - Visual org chart comparison
6. **Document your decision** - Note why you chose one scenario over others
7. **Share comparisons early** - Get stakeholder input before finalizing
8. **Update scenarios based on feedback** - Refine and re-compare

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Comparing scenarios with different effective dates** - Makes cost/headcount comparison misleading

❌ **Comparing too many scenarios at once** - 5+ scenarios is overwhelming

❌ **Not naming scenarios clearly** - "Scenario 1 vs Scenario 2" is confusing

❌ **Only comparing cost** - Structure and complexity matter too

❌ **Not exporting comparison** - Hard to reference later or share with others

❌ **Choosing based on cost alone** - Cheapest option may not be best organizationally

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** I can't compare more than 2 scenarios.
- **Solution:** Use arrow buttons at bottom to add up to 4 total scenarios.

**Problem:** Comparison view is too cluttered.
- **Solution:** Remove scenarios (click X on column) until you have 2-3 for clarity.

**Problem:** Cost comparison doesn't show difference.
- **Solution:** Ensure scenarios have different changes. Two identical scenarios won't show differences.

**Problem:** Can't export comparison.
- **Solution:** Check permissions. Export may be restricted by admin settings.

**Problem:** Org charts look identical in comparison view.
- **Solution:** Zoom in or scroll to specific parts. Top-level may look same while details differ.

**Problem:** I want to compare Main Org to a scenario.
- **Solution:** Select Main Org as one of the comparison options (it appears in scenario list).
