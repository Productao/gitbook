# Advanced Position Interactions

### Overview

This guide covers advanced interactions available through the **three-dot menu** when hovering over a position card. These actions provide additional flexibility for managing and structuring your organization.

### Advanced Position Actions

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-11 at 6.20.44 PM.png" alt="" width="336"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

#### 1. Selecting Individuals or Teams

* Use **Command + K (Mac) / Control + K (Windows)** for quick multi-selection.
* Select an **entire team** to apply bulk changes.

#### 2. Changing Manager Assignments

* Instead of **dragging and dropping**, use the **Change Manager** option.
* Assign a position or team under a new leader without manually repositioning it.

#### 3. Adding a Backfill

* Closes an **existing position** and **creates a new position** to replace it.
* Useful for **succession planning** and ensuring vacancies are properly filled.

#### 4. Creating Dotted Line Reporting

* Establishes **matrix reporting structures**.
* Allows a position to **report to multiple managers** for cross-functional collaboration.

#### 5. Setting a Position as a Root

* Makes a position **a top-level role** (e.g., like a CEO).
* Allows for **multiple root positions** within the org chart.

#### 6. Commenting on a Position

* Attach **notes and comments** to specific positions.
* Tag collaborators to **share feedback or discuss changes** in real-time.

By leveraging these **advanced actions**, you can optimize **org structure, succession planning, and collaboration** within Agentnoon.
