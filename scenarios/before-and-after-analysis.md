# Before and After Analysis

### Overview

This guide explains how the **Workforce Hub** in Agentnoon provides **before-and-after comparisons** to visualize the impact of scenario changes on key workforce metrics.

#### 1. Navigating to the Workforce Hub

1. Inside a **scenario**, go to the **Org Chart** dropdown.
2. Select **Workforce Hub** to access workforce analytics.

#### 2. Understanding Before & After Comparisons

1. In a scenario, **all charts** display a **before-and-after view** to highlight changes.
2. Example:
   * **Headcount Comparison** shows **where increases or reductions** have occurred.
   * Similar **before-and-after** views are available for:
     * **Headcount Cost**
     * **Spans & Layers**
     * **Other workforce metrics**

#### 3. Customizing the View

1. By default, **Workforce Hub charts show changes** side by side.
2. To modify the view:
   * Click the **drop-down toggle** in the chart settings.
   * Choose to display **only "before" or "after" data**, or keep the side-by-side comparison.

By leveraging **Workforce Hub comparisons**, you can **analyze workforce changes, measure organizational impact, and gain insights into scenario adjustments** within Agentnoon.
