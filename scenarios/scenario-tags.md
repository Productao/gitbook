# Scenario Tags

### Overview

This guide covers how to organize scenarios with tags. Scenario tags help you group plans by team, department, or planning timeline - making it easier to keep your workspace organized and collaborative.

### Creating a New Tag

To create a new tag for your scenarios,&#x20;

1. Navigate to `⋯` next to any scenario
2. Click **Change Tag**
3. Name your **new tag** and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to create it. \
