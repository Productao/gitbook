# Notifications

Agentnoon sends notifications to keep you informed of key platform events, delivered in-app (notification bell) and by email for time-sensitive events.

## What Triggers Notifications

* **Approval requests** — When a scenario is submitted for your approval. Delivered immediately via email and in-app.
* **Approval status updates** — When a scenario you submitted is approved or rejected. Delivered immediately.
* **Invitations** — When a scenario is shared with you. Delivered immediately by email.
* **Account creation** — When a new Agentnoon account is created for you by an admin. Delivered by email with login instructions.

## What Does NOT Trigger Notifications

* Changes made inside a scenario by other collaborators
* Comments added to a scenario or position
* Presence of other users in a scenario

To coordinate on scenario changes, use the comments feature or communicate outside Agentnoon.

## Viewing Notifications

Click the notification bell in the top-right corner to see recent in-app notifications.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                               | Solution                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Not receiving approval request emails | Check spam; verify your email address in your profile; contact admin |
| Not receiving invitation email        | Check spam; ask the scenario owner to reshare                        |
| In-app notification bell not updating | Refresh the page or log out and back in                              |
| Missing approval status update        | Check the scenario's Approvals panel directly                        |
